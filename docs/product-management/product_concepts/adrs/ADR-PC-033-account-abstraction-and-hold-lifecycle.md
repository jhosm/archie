## Context

**In plain English.** [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) decided babelstone is "a core product & account ledger" and stated the load-bearing posture: *a balance is a fold over the movements posted to an account* (§95). [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) then named the atom that fold sums — the single-sided `Movement` — and its 2026-06-23 amendment named the `IMovementBearing` seam that lets one spine-owned projector find movements on any family's event. What is still missing is the **thing the fold produces**: the **account** itself, and — for a transactional account — the split between the **accounting balance** (what has actually posted) and the **available balance** (what is spendable right now, net of money earmarked by approved-but-unsettled authorizations). That earmarked amount is a **hold**, and a hold has its own little lifecycle: it is *placed* when an authorization is approved, *captured* when the matching settlement arrives, or *expires* if it times out. PC-030 deferred this whole Account model to its heaviest families (the `credit_card` slice and the `conta à ordem`, roadmap shapes 3–4). This ADR decides that model **now** — the contract, not the engine code — so the shape-3 family lands against a fixed Account abstraction instead of re-deriving it.

**Which boundary this crosses.** Not a wire boundary to an external system — an **intra-engine contract** between the family-agnostic spine and each family's projection. The spine offers a generic Account abstraction (a balance is a fold over `Movement`s; a transactional account additionally folds holds); a family declares *its* projection state *is* an account through a small seam, exactly as it declares erasure through `IErasable` ([ADR-PC-004 A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)) and movement-bearing events through `IMovementBearing` ([ADR-PC-032 A1](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)). The real-time authorization path ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md), stages 3–5) is the in-engine reader of `available balance`.

**Why a contract-shape ADR, and why now.** This is a boundary contract two parties build against independently — the spine (the generic balance/hold fold + the authorization seam) and each family (its account-typed projection state, its hold-bearing events) — so it takes the [ADR-PC-000 §D3](./ADR-PC-000-namespace-and-contract-shape-framework.md) six-slot contract-shape template, not an F1/F2 tool pick (nothing is bought). It is pulled forward of the shape-3 family — the same move [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) made for the `Movement` atom — because fixing the Account abstraction *before* the first transactional family de-risks it: `personal_loan` and `term_deposit` already fold a balance (a degenerate account with no available/accounting split and no holds), so naming the general shape now lets the shape-3 `conta à ordem` be the *first instance* of a fixed abstraction rather than the *definition* of an unfixed one.

**Scope — the model, not the engine.** This ADR decides the **Account contract**: the account-as-fold posture, the two-balance split, the hold lifecycle and its three transitions, and the seam by which a family declares its state an account. The **engine implementation** — the `IAccount`/`IHoldable` interfaces, the generic balance/available-balance projector, the `FundsHeld`/hold events, the authorization decider, the migrations — is **out of scope** and is a tracked follow-up (the engine half of bd `babelstone-hkwp.3`). The real-time authorization *technique* (synchronous vs asynchronous) stays [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s deferred runtime ADR; this ADR commits only the *model* the technique will serve.

## Decision

### Every engine-owned aggregate is an `Account`: a balance is a deterministic fold over the `Movement`s posted to it; a transactional account additionally folds a **hold** ledger, so its **available balance = accounting balance − Σ active holds**. A family declares its projection state *is* an account through a spine-owned seam (`IAccount`-style, the [ADR-PC-004 A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) / [ADR-PC-032 A1](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) pattern); the engine owns the generic fold, the family supplies only its state. **No balance is ever a stored mutable number; both balances and every hold are pure, rebuildable folds.**

The six contract slots:

1. **Payload shape — the Account abstraction (three parts).**
   - **An account is the fold target.** Every engine-owned aggregate (a deposit, a loan, a current account, a card account) **is an account**: a stream of `Movement`s ([ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)) keyed by `account_ref`, whose **accounting balance** is the deterministic fold `Σ(Credit) − Σ(Debit)` over the movements posted to it ([ADR-PC-030 §95](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)). The account is not a new stored type with a balance column — it is a **role a projection state plays**, declared through the seam (part 3). Term deposit and personal loan are **degenerate accounts**: one balance, no holds — they already fold this way, so naming the abstraction reclassifies them, it does not change them.
   - **The two-balance split (transactional accounts only).** A transactional account ([ADR-PC-030 §48](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md), the `conta à ordem` / card account) additionally distinguishes the **accounting balance** (what has posted) from the **available balance** (what is spendable now). Their gap is the **held amount**. `available balance` is **not a stored field** but a second fold: `accounting balance − Σ(active holds)`. A non-transactional account's available balance is trivially its accounting balance (no holds), so the split is *uniform* — the same fold, with an empty hold set.
   - **The hold.** A `Hold` is a spine value object — `hold_id` (the dedup/correlation key, slot 4), `account_ref` (the opaque account, never PII — [ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)), `amount` (`Money`, integer cents, [ADR-PC-010 §P2](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)), `value_date`, and a lifecycle `state` (part of slot 2). It carries **no family-typed shape** and **no PII**, so [`ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`](./commitment-catalogue.md) ([ADR-PC-021 §P2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)) holds. Holds are recorded by **engine-declared, family-agnostic cross-cutting events** in the `Babelstone.Engine.CrossCuttingEvents` spine — `HoldPlaced` / `HoldCaptured` / `HoldExpired` (the `FundsHeld`-family the spine already names as planned), under the synthetic `operations` aggregate_type, the same posture as `PackVersionMigrated` / `PersonalDataErasureRequested` ([ADR-PC-004 A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) / [ADR-IC-017 §P4](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-017-integration-event-promotion-criterion.md)). The concrete Avro shapes are authored with the shape-3 family and are the **contract-reviewer**'s lane — this ADR fixes the *contract*, not the wire bytes.

2. **Semantics — the hold lifecycle is three pure transitions.** A hold asserts *"N cents on `account_ref` are earmarked by an approved-but-unsettled authorization, from `value_date`."* Its lifecycle is exactly three events, each a pure fold ([ADR-PC-030 §48/§176](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)):
   - **`HoldPlaced`** — an authorization was approved (stage 5 of [ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)): the funds-and-rules decider read `available balance`, confirmed it covers the amount under pack rules (limits, *descoberto autorizado*), and appended `HoldPlaced`. From this event's sequence forward the hold is **active** and lowers `available balance` — which is what makes concurrent authorizations safe without locking ([ADR-PC-030 §48](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md): the first debit's `HoldPlaced` lowers the available balance before the second authorization is evaluated, against the append-only log).
   - **`HoldCaptured`** — the matching settlement/capture arrived (an `Observed` `Movement`, [ADR-PC-032 A4](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)): the hold is released and a posting `Movement` lands. The hold leaves the active set (stops reducing `available balance`); the captured `Movement` moves the **accounting** balance. A capture may be **partial** — a `HoldCaptured` for less than the held amount releases the remainder (a residual risk, below).
   - **`HoldExpired`** — the hold timed out before capture: it leaves the active set, restoring `available balance`, with **no posting** (no money moved). Expiry is driven by a **projection-derived read** over the active-hold set against a value-date horizon, **never a clock-manufactured engine event** ([ADR-PC-023](./ADR-PC-023-temporal-signals-projection-derived.md)) — the `HoldExpired` fact is appended by an operator/command-shell action reading that projection, keeping the fold pure and replay deterministic.

   The carrying lifecycle stays the family's: a family event may *cause* a hold (the family decider decided the authorization), but the **hold record is the cross-cutting fact** the generic projector folds — the engine knows "this account has an active hold of N," the family knows what authorization placed it.

3. **Ordering and delivery.** **Per-account ordering**: holds and movements against one `account_ref` are folded in append order, inheriting the outbox's per-aggregate FIFO ([ADR-IC-004](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-004-outbox-pattern-mechanism.md)) — `HoldPlaced` must be ordered before its `HoldCaptured`/`HoldExpired` (a capture/expiry of an unplaced hold is a fold error, surfaced not silently absorbed). Because the balances are **folds, not stored numbers**, a rebuild re-derives both balances and the active-hold set identically from the stream (replay determinism, [ADR-PC-010](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)). There is **no external delivery guarantee in this slot** — the Account abstraction is intra-engine; the only cross-boundary delivery is the `Observed` capture that drives `HoldCaptured`, whose at-least-once + dedup contract is [ADR-PC-032 A5](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)'s, not restated here.

4. **Idempotency — `hold_id` for the hold, the existing `CommandId` for the authorization append.** A hold's lifecycle is keyed by **`hold_id`**: `HoldPlaced`/`HoldCaptured`/`HoldExpired` for one authorization all carry the same `hold_id`, so a redelivered capture or a replayed expiry folds at most once (a second `HoldCaptured` for a `hold_id` already captured is a no-op, surfaced as a reconciliation signal, not a double-release). The **authorization append** (the command that produced `HoldPlaced`) is made idempotent by the **`CommandId`** the originating command already carries ([ADR-PC-029 slot 4](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md)) — a replayed authorize command returns the original `commit_sequence` with no second `HoldPlaced`. These are **two keys for two seams** (the hold's own lifecycle vs the command append), neither derived from the other — the same two-key discipline [ADR-PC-032 slot 4](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) drew. **Uniqueness window**: the life of the account's stream (holds are immutable facts retained like the events that carry them).

5. **Error model (the load-bearing slot) — the authorization decision is gated; the balance fold never is.**
   - **The funds-and-rules authorization is gated** ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) stages 3–4): a debit attempt that exceeds `available balance` under pack rules is **refused** — the decider appends a refusal, **not** a `HoldPlaced`, and the caller gets `declined`. This is a pure read-state-and-append decision (no external call, no clock), so it stays a deterministic fold even though its *answer* gates the authorization path. The gate blocks the **authorization**, never the recording of facts.
   - **The balance and hold folds are never gated** — recording a `Movement` or a hold event is part of a local append, committed atomically with the event; computing `available balance` cannot fail independently of folding the stream. Recording a fact never blocks on a balance check (only the *authorization decider* consults the balance, and that is before its append).
   - **The hold `amount > available balance` invariant is enforced at authorization, not at fold time.** The fold trusts its input stream (a `HoldPlaced` that should never have been appended over-earmarks the account, a reconciliation signal); the decider is where over-authorization is *prevented*, by reading the available-balance fold first. This keeps the fold pure and the prevention in the one impure place that already reads state.
   - **The GL stays untouched** ([ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md)): a hold is an **engine-internal earmark**, never a GL posting — only the *captured* `Movement` is a business fact the GL may book under its own model. A hold placement/expiry emits no GL signal.

6. **Ownership and versioning.** The **engine spine** owns the Account abstraction — the `IAccount`/`IHoldable`-style seam, the generic balance + available-balance + active-hold folds, and the `operations.HoldPlaced/HoldCaptured/HoldExpired` cross-cutting event records — **all name no family** (opaque `account_ref`, generic `Money`/`hold_id`/`value_date`), so the `family → engine` arrow stays one-way and no family-typed shape enters the engine set ([ADR-PC-021 §P2/§A5](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)). Each **family** owns which of its projection states *is* an account (it implements the seam) and which lifecycle events place holds (the family *content*, in `families/**`). The **pack** owns the limit/*descoberto autorizado* rules the authorization decider evaluates ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) — pack-grammar expansion). Breaking changes to the cross-cutting hold events follow forward-only evolution ([ADR-IC-002](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-002-schema-format-and-registry.md)) with a consumer-driven contract test ([ADR-IC-009](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-009-testing-infrastructure.md)) once a hold event is bus-promoted.

**Rejected: a stored, mutable balance (and a stored available balance).** Caching either balance as a column re-introduces a value the log and the projection can disagree about — the double-counting / shadow-balance failure mode [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) and [ADR-PC-030 §95](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) forbid, and breaks replay determinism. Both balances stay folds; a snapshot ([ADR-PC-003](./ADR-PC-003-postgresql-snapshots.md)) is a *recomputable* optimisation, never the source of truth. **Rejected: a family-typed `Account` base class families inherit.** A shared cross-family `Account` type in the spine would be a family-aware shape ([ADR-PC-021 §A5](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) forbids it) and a shared cross-family *application* layer ([ADR-PC-021 §D1](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) forbids it); the family-declared seam (a family implements an interface on its *own* state) is the established conformant alternative. **Rejected: holds as family events.** A per-family `DepositFundsHeld` / `AccountFundsHeld` would re-derive the same cross-cutting fact per family and collide on the simple-name-keyed codec — exactly the failure [ADR-PC-004 A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) hit with erasure; the cross-cutting `operations.HoldPlaced` removes the duplication at the root. **Rejected: clock-driven hold expiry.** An engine scheduler emitting `HoldExpired` when a timer fires would manufacture an event from a clock — [ADR-PC-023](./ADR-PC-023-temporal-signals-projection-derived.md) forbids it; expiry is a projection-derived read.

## Consequences

**What this makes easier:**

1. **The shape-3 `conta à ordem` lands against a fixed abstraction.** The hardest family ([ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) shape 4) builds on a decided two-balance/hold model instead of inventing it — naming it now de-risks it, the same payoff [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) bought by naming the `Movement` atom early.
2. **One balance discipline across every family.** Liability, closed-end asset, revolving asset, and transactional account all fold a balance the same way; the transactional ones merely add the hold fold. The reference topology ([ADR-PC-030 §S2](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)) is demonstrably *one* kernel expressing every shape.
3. **Concurrent authorization is safe without locking.** Because `HoldPlaced` lowers `available balance` on the append-only log before the next authorization is evaluated, double-spend is prevented by construction ([ADR-PC-030 §48](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)), no distributed lock.
4. **Replay stays total.** Both balances and the active-hold set are rebuildable folds — a discard-and-rebuild drill reproduces them exactly ([ADR-PC-010](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md) / [ADR-PC-003](./ADR-PC-003-postgresql-snapshots.md)).

**What this makes harder or impossible:**

1. **No fast stored balance read without a projection.** `available balance` is always a fold (or a recomputable snapshot/projection of one) — a hot-path read needs the read model, never a mutable column. Accepted: it is the price of replay-correctness and the no-shadow-balance rule.
2. **The hold-expiry horizon must be a projection read, not a timer.** Operators/commands drive `HoldExpired` off a projection over the active-hold set — more plumbing than a scheduler, but the only shape that keeps the fold pure ([ADR-PC-023](./ADR-PC-023-temporal-signals-projection-derived.md)).
3. **Partial capture and over-authorization are correctness-sensitive and must be reconciled.** The fold trusts its stream; the decider prevents over-earmarking — so the gap between them is a reconciliation surface a family must own (residual risk below).

## Residual risks (what this contract does **not** commit to)

- **The engine implementation is not decided here.** The `IAccount`/`IHoldable` seam interfaces, the generic balance/available-balance/active-hold projector, the concrete `HoldPlaced`/`HoldCaptured`/`HoldExpired` event records + Avro, the authorization decider, and the read-model migrations are the **engine half of bd `babelstone-hkwp.3`** — a tracked follow-up. This ADR fixes only the contract they realise. The concrete hold-event wire shapes are the **contract-reviewer**'s lane, authored with the shape-3 family.
- **The real-time authorization *technique* stays deferred.** Whether the authorize-style debit is a synchronous call ([ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) shape) or an asynchronous/reactive design is [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s deferred runtime ADR — this ADR commits the *model* (available balance as a fold, the hold lifecycle), not the latency mechanism.
- **Hold reconciliation is owned by the transactional family, not fixed here** ([ADR-PC-030 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)): hold expiry vs late capture, partial captures, reversals, and re-presentments are correctness-sensitive and are the `conta à ordem` / `credit_card` family ADRs' to specify within this abstraction. This ADR fixes the *three lifecycle transitions and their idempotency key*, not each family's reconciliation policy.
- **The no-engine-shadow-balance rule is untouched through v1–v3** ([ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md)): the engine holds no balance for an account the legacy Core authoritatively owns. The Account abstraction applies to **engine-owned** balances (deposit, loan, and the v4-migrated `conta à ordem`); it is not a licence to shadow a legacy-owned current account before v4 ([ADR-PC-030 §Decision item 4](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)).
- **Statement issuance is out of scope** — an issued statement is a **sealed event**, not a replayable projection ([ADR-PC-030 §176](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)); it is a separate family-owned design, not part of this Account contract.
- **Pack-grammar expansion for limits/*descoberto autorizado* is named, not authored** ([ADR-PC-030 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)): the authorization decider evaluates pack rules at stage 4, but the [ADR-PC-006](./ADR-PC-006-cue-schema-language.md)/[ADR-PC-007](./ADR-PC-007-signed-yaml-oci-pack.md) grammar that expresses them is a separate change.
