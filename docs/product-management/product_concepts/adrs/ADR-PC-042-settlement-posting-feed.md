## Context

**In plain English.** A current account (a *conta à ordem*) is a hub that money constantly flows through: a salary lands, a mortgage direct-debit leaves, a transfer arrives, and — once a debit or credit card is attached — every card purchase, refund, and fee eventually settles against it. babelstone's engine does **not** move any of that money. It never talks to the card network, never clears a direct debit, never settles a transfer ([ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) draws the boundary right there: the engine decides and records, it never *physically moves* money). So all of those already-cleared movements have to **arrive from outside**, on a feed, for the engine to write them into the account's balance. This ADR is the contract for that feed: what each posting on it looks like, in what order they come, how a duplicate delivery is ignored, what happens when a record is malformed, who owns the schema — and, because the engine does not control the feed, **who notices when a posting goes missing and what they do about it**.

Nothing here is bought or installed. The engine team is committing to a payload shape and an ownership model that two families and one feed owner will build against independently — so this is a **contract-shape ADR** on the [ADR-PC-000 §D3](./ADR-PC-000-namespace-and-contract-shape-framework.md) six-slot template (payload, semantics, ordering & delivery, idempotency/dedup, error model, ownership/versioning), plus the seventh concern this feed forces: **gap-detection ownership**. There is no F1/F2 tool-selection table.

**Why now, and its relationship to [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md).** [ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) named "the settlement/posting feed … a future contract-shape ADR" and deferred it. [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) — the `Movement` primitive, pulled forward two product shapes early to close a settlement-atomicity pain — had to name the `Observed` direction of that feed to make its `origin = Observed | Originated` split coherent, and its amendments **§A4–§A6** (2026-06-23) sketched the feed's contract slots inside the Movement ADR: the family-agnostic `operations.MovementObserved` recording event, the `IMovementBearing` fold seam, the `movement_id` dedup key, and the reconciliation-flow-2 gap-detection ownership. Those amendments explicitly left the feed **"specified-but-dormant"** because v1 has no `Observed` producer, and flagged that a live producer arrives with **shape 3** — the transactional families. That moment is now: the `conta à ordem` family (ADR-PC-037) is being authored as the *first* [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) transactional instance, and the `credit_card` family ([ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md)) that follows it **reuses the same feed**. This ADR is the dedicated [§P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) home those two families' epics reference by id ([ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md) Related: "the settlement/posting-feed contract-shape ADR it owns (bd `babelstone-30hf.5`)"). **It honours [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md); it neither amends nor supersedes it.** §Decision slot 2 and §A4–§A6 remain binding as written — this ADR consolidates the feed contract those amendments provisionally carried into the standalone home §P3 always intended, keeps every one of their load-bearing choices byte-consistent (same event name, same dedup key, same reconciliation invariant), and fills the two things a dedicated home can now add that a Movement-primitive amendment could not: the **two-family shared-consumer** model (one feed, current-account-owned, card-consumed) and a **concrete, engine-side gap-detection mechanism** (a per-`account_ref` feed sequence the engine checks locally) beyond §A5's reconciliation-only sketch.

**Which boundary this crosses.** A wire boundary the engine does **not** own the far side of. On one side, upstream capture/clearing/settlement systems that have *already moved the money*; on the other, the engine's ACL ingestion path, which records each already-cleared movement as a `MovementObserved` event and folds it into the account balance. Contrast the **`Originated`** direction ([ADR-PC-032 slot 2](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)): there the engine *decided* the movement and drives a gated cash leg to effect it. Here the money already moved; there is **no cash leg to drive** — only a fact to record.

## Decision

### The settlement/posting feed delivers already-cleared money movements as `Observed` `Movement`s ([ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)), each recorded by the engine as a cross-cutting, family-agnostic `operations.MovementObserved` event and folded into the `account_ref`-keyed balance — **recorded-only, never a cash leg**. Delivery is at-least-once with consumer-side dedup on the feed's `movement_id`; the balance fold is order-insensitive; the feed owner (the `conta à ordem` family) owns the schema **and** the gap: the engine **detects and surfaces** a missing posting against a per-`account_ref` feed sequence, but **never invents** the missing fact.

The six contract slots, plus gap-detection ownership:

1. **Payload shape — one already-cleared `Movement` per feed record, recorded as `operations.MovementObserved`.** Each feed record carries exactly one [ADR-PC-032 slot 1](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) `Movement` with `origin = Observed`, plus the feed-level fields the contract needs:
   - `movement_id` — the **feed-assigned, stable** dedup identifier (slot 4). The feed owns its allocation; it is opaque to the engine, **never PII** ([ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)).
   - `account_ref` — the engine-owned account the movement posts to (opaque reference, **never** an IBAN/name/NIF).
   - `direction` — `Debit | Credit`.
   - `amount` — `Money` (integer cents; crosses from `decimal` at most once, [ADR-PC-010 §P1–§P2](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)).
   - `value_date` — the economic date (`valid_time`), never wall-clock.
   - `reason` — a closed, **family-declared** movement-code *string* (`salary_credit`, `direct_debit`, `transfer_in`, `card_capture`, `card_refund`, `scheme_fee`, …); an opaque token to the spine, **not** a family-typed shape.
   - `feed_sequence` — a **per-`account_ref` monotonic** sequence the feed stamps on each record it emits for that account, the hook that makes gap-detection cheap and local (slot 7).

   The engine records the movement as **`operations.MovementObserved`** — a **cross-cutting, engine-declared** event in the [`Babelstone.Engine.CrossCuttingEvents`](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) spine, the same family-agnostic posture as `PackVersionMigrated` / `LegacyInstanceObserved` ([ADR-PC-032 §A4](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) / [ADR-IC-017 §P4](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-017-integration-event-promotion-criterion.md)). It **names no family** (so [`ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`](./commitment-catalogue.md) holds) and **implements `IMovementBearing`** (the [ADR-PC-032 §A1](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) seam), so the **same** spine-owned movement projector folds it into the `account_ref`-keyed ledger with **no special-casing** of the Observed source or of *which* consumer family the account belongs to. The concrete Avro/CUE wire shape is authored with the first live producer (shape 3) and is the **contract-reviewer**'s lane, governed like every emitted contract (registry + generated reference) — what this ADR fixes is the *slots*, not the wire bytes.

2. **Semantics — an already-cleared fact, recorded and folded, with no cash leg.** A feed record asserts *"N cents already moved `direction` against `account_ref` on `value_date`, because `reason`; the feed calls it `movement_id`."* The money moved **upstream**; the engine **records** the movement and folds it into the account's accounting balance — there is **no cash leg to drive** ([ADR-PC-032 slot 2](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md), unchanged), because the engine is not the mover. Both consumer families read the **same** feed and the **same** event: for `conta à ordem` an Observed movement is a salary credit, a bill debit, a transfer; for `credit_card` it is a cleared card leg (purchase, refund, fee) posting to the card account. The feed is **agnostic to which family owns the account** — the family identity lives in the account, not the posting. An Observed credit and an Observed debit both fold; a card refund is simply an Observed `Credit`, a purchase an Observed `Debit`.

3. **Ordering and delivery — at-least-once, per-`account_ref` order, order-insensitive fold.** Delivery is **at-least-once** with consumer-side dedup (slot 4). The feed makes **no cross-account global-order promise**; per-`account_ref` order is the engine's **append order of the `MovementObserved` events** it records, inheriting the outbox per-aggregate FIFO ([ADR-IC-004](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-004-outbox-pattern-mechanism.md)). The `account_ref`-keyed **balance fold is order-insensitive** — a balance is a *sum* of signed movements ([ADR-PC-030 §95](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) / [ADR-PC-010](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)), so out-of-order arrival **within** an account self-heals on rebuild; the *accounting* balance is eventually consistent and always converges to the same value regardless of arrival order. (The one place order/lag is *visible* is the real-time `available balance` a debit authorization reads — a residual, below: a not-yet-arrived Observed debit transiently over-states available funds; the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) hold lifecycle, not this feed, covers the authorized-not-yet-cleared window.)

4. **Idempotency / dedup — the feed's `movement_id`, deduped consumer-side.** The dedup key is the feed-assigned `movement_id` (slot 1), deduped **on the engine (consumer) side** in the ACL ingestion path against the existing `inbound_event_dedup` store ([ADR-IC-012 §P1](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-012-anti-corruption-layer-implementation.md)) — the **identical mechanism** the [ADR-PC-017 batch ingest](./ADR-PC-017-legacy-batch-ingest-contract.md) uses for `LegacyInstanceObserved`. A redelivered feed record produces **no second `MovementObserved`** and therefore no double-fold into the balance. The feed makes **no exactly-once promise**; effectively-once is the consumer's dedup, not the transport's. **Uniqueness window**: the life of the instance (movements are immutable facts, retained like the events that carry them — [ADR-PC-032 slot 4](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md), unchanged).

5. **Error model (the load-bearing slot) — nothing to gate, fail-loud on malformed.** An Observed movement has **no cash leg** (the money already moved), so there is **nothing to gate and nothing to compensate**. Recording it is part of the engine's local append and **cannot be gated independently of the append**. A **malformed or non-conforming feed record** — unknown `account_ref`, absent `movement_id`, a `Money` that does not parse, a `direction` outside the closed enum — is **rejected fail-loud at the ACL ingestion boundary** and **never half-recorded**, the same posture as the [ADR-PC-017](./ADR-PC-017-legacy-batch-ingest-contract.md) record-level rejection; the rejected record surfaces as an operational alert on the same machinery the batch feed uses, resolved by the feed owner re-emitting a well-formed record (it carries the same `movement_id`, so the retry dedups against nothing and records once). There is **no compensation path** — there is nothing to undo. The GL is untouched ([ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md)): an Observed movement is a business fact the engine records; the GL *posts* its own balanced journal entry from the raw event, and never receives a `Movement`.

6. **Ownership and versioning — the feed owner owns the schema; the engine owns the record; the card family is a second consumer.** The **feed owner is the `conta à ordem` family** (ADR-PC-037): it owns the feed's wire schema, its `movement_id`/`feed_sequence` allocation, and its gap-detection (slot 7). The **engine spine** owns the `operations.MovementObserved` record and its `IMovementBearing` fold — both name no family ([ADR-PC-021 §P2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)). The **`credit_card` family** ([ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md)) is a **second consumer of the one feed**, not the owner of a second feed — a card capture is an Observed movement on the same seam; the card family owns only its own `reason` vocabulary and which of its accounts the postings target. Each **family** owns its `reason` codes (the family *content*). Breaking changes to the feed follow **forward-only evolution** ([ADR-IC-002](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-002-schema-format-and-registry.md) / [§09](../../integration_concepts/09-long-term-schema-evolution.md)) with a **consumer-driven contract test** (Pact, [ADR-IC-009](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-009-testing-infrastructure.md)) on the feed seam — and, because there are **two** consumer families, the Pact suite carries **both** families' expectations, so a feed change that would break either fails at CI, not in production.

7. **Gap-detection ownership (the seventh concern — a feed the engine does not control).** *Who notices a missing posting, and how.* The **gap is the feed owner's to own** — to *classify* (a dropped delivery vs. an upstream clearance that was never emitted) and to *close* (re-emit the missing record). The **engine's role is to make gaps detectable and to surface them, never to synthesise the missing fact**:
   - **Local detection — the per-`account_ref` `feed_sequence`.** Because the feed stamps a per-account **monotonic** `feed_sequence` (slot 1), the engine can spot a **hole locally**: a jump in the sequence for an `account_ref` (record 7 arrives after record 5, with 6 never seen within a bounded reordering window) is a *candidate gap* the engine raises without needing the feed owner's ledger. This is the concrete mechanism this ADR adds beyond [ADR-PC-032 §A5](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)'s reconciliation-only sketch — cheap, account-local, and continuous rather than only end-of-day.
   - **Authoritative reconciliation — the flow-2 invariant.** The engine's `account_ref`-keyed movement ledger (built from the cumulative `MovementObserved` stream) is reconciled against the feed owner's own record of cleared movements — the **same reconciliation-flow-2 invariant** ([ADR-PC-017](./ADR-PC-017-legacy-batch-ingest-contract.md) / [coexistence §7.3](../feature-design-strangler-fig-coexistence.md)): every movement the engine recorded must map to a cleared movement on the feed side, and vice versa.
   - **The engine never invents the fact.** On either signal — a local sequence hole or a reconciliation divergence — the engine **surfaces** the mismatch as an operational alert; the feed owner **classifies and re-emits**. The engine does not manufacture a balance-affecting `Movement` from a suspected gap, because a fabricated posting would corrupt a fold that must stay a faithful sum of *observed* facts ([ADR-PC-032 §A5](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md), unchanged — restated concrete here). The `Originated` direction keeps its **own** gap-detection — the [ADR-PC-016 reconciliation flow 1](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md), every movement matched to a Core-journal counterpart — untouched by this ADR.

**Rejected: a second, card-owned capture feed.** Giving `credit_card` its own capture feed duplicates the schema, the dedup store, and the gap-detection for a movement that is structurally identical to any other Observed posting — a card capture is just an Observed `Debit`. One feed, two consumer families, keeps a single reconciliation seam and a single Pact home. **Rejected: the engine synthesises a missing posting from a detected gap.** Fabricating a `Movement` to "fill" a sequence hole makes the balance fold no longer a faithful sum of observed facts and hides a real upstream failure behind a plausible-looking number; detection + surfacing is the honest boundary, backfill is the feed owner's. **Rejected: cross-account global ordering on the feed.** A global sequence across every account would force the feed owner into a total order it has no reason to guarantee and buys nothing — the balance fold is per-account and order-insensitive; per-`account_ref` monotonicity is all gap-detection needs. **Rejected: an exactly-once transport promise.** At-least-once + consumer-side `movement_id` dedup is the same effectively-once posture every ACL-ingested feed already takes ([ADR-PC-017](./ADR-PC-017-legacy-batch-ingest-contract.md) / [ADR-IC-012](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-012-anti-corruption-layer-implementation.md)); demanding exactly-once of an external feed the engine does not control is unenforceable and needless. **Rejected: gate the recording on the cash leg (treat Observed like Originated).** There is no cash leg — the money already moved; gating a *recording* on a settlement that already happened is incoherent.

## Consequences

**What this makes easier:**

1. **One feed, two families, one reconciliation seam.** The `conta à ordem` and `credit_card` families build against a single Observed-movement contract; a card capture reuses the current account's feed, dedup store, gap-detection, and Pact home rather than re-deciding them. Adding a third transactional family later inherits the seam.
2. **The balance is always a faithful, rebuildable sum of observed facts.** Order-insensitive folding + consumer-side dedup means the accounting balance converges to the same value on replay regardless of delivery order or redelivery — no shadow state, no de-dup bookkeeping in the fold ([ADR-PC-010](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)).
3. **A missing posting is caught, not silently absorbed.** The per-`account_ref` `feed_sequence` turns "did we drop a record?" into a cheap local check, backstopped by the flow-2 reconciliation — a gap becomes an alert, not a quietly wrong balance.
4. **The engine stays a recorder, not a mover.** No card-network, clearing, or settlement machinery crosses the boundary ([ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)); the feed is the only channel by which already-cleared money reaches the ledger.

**What this makes harder or impossible:**

1. **The accounting balance is eventually consistent, not instantaneous.** A movement is in the balance only once its feed record arrives and is recorded; a lagging feed means the *accounting* balance trails reality. Accepted: real-time spendability is the `available balance` (accounting − Σ holds), and the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) hold lifecycle — not this feed — covers the authorized-not-yet-cleared window.
2. **Gap-closure is an operational duty split across two parties.** The engine surfaces; the feed owner classifies and backfills — a standing reconciliation process, not a one-off. That is the honest cost of a feed the engine does not control.
3. **The feed owner must allocate a stable `movement_id` and a per-account `feed_sequence`.** Real work this contract pushes onto the counterparty by design — without both, dedup and local gap-detection degrade to reconciliation-only.

## Residual risks (what this contract does **not** commit to)

- **The concrete Avro/CUE wire shape and the feed transport are named, not authored.** They land with the first live producer (shape 3, the `conta à ordem` family) and are the **contract-reviewer**'s lane, exactly as [ADR-PC-032 §Residual-risks / §A6](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) deferred. The feed stays **specified-but-dormant** until that producer exists — nothing here mandates a v1 build.
- **The bounded-reordering window for local gap-detection is a calibration, not a constant.** "How long the engine waits for `feed_sequence` N before raising a candidate gap" is an operational threshold tuned under real feed latency — the same accept-now-calibrate-at-production posture as the [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md) reconciliation thresholds; until then the sequence-hole alert may fire on merely-late (not lost) records.
- **The feed owner's classification and backfill policy is theirs, not the engine's.** How a divergence is triaged (dropped delivery vs. unrecorded upstream clearance) and how a missing record is re-sourced is a feed-owner/operating-bank deliverable; this ADR fixes the *shape and the ownership split*, not the runbook.
- **No real-time authorization guarantee is made by the feed.** The feed is eventually consistent; a debit-authorization correctness claim is [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) + [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s (holds), not this contract's.
- **The `Originated` direction is untouched.** This ADR governs only the `Observed` posting feed; the engine-initiated gated cash leg, its idempotency, and its flow-1 reconciliation stay [ADR-PC-032 slots 2–5](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) / [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md).
