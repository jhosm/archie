## Context

**In plain English.** This adds the **fourth and most fundamental family** to the engine: the *conta à ordem* — a Portuguese **current / demand account**. Where a term deposit takes money *in* and locks it to maturity, and a personal loan pays a lump sum *out* and amortizes it to zero, a current account holds money **available on demand** and is the **hub the other three settle against**: salaries land in it, bills leave it, a deposit's interest and a loan's installments move through it. Its defining trick — the one that makes it a *transactional* account — is that it tracks **two** balances: the **accounting balance** (what has actually posted) and the **available balance** (what is spendable right now, after subtracting money earmarked by approved-but-unsettled authorizations). The earmark is a **hold** (PT *cativo*), and answering "can this debit go through?" against the available balance, **in real time**, is the engine's job for this family.

Crucially, **most of that model is already decided** by the time this family lands. [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) fixed the **Account abstraction** — every engine-owned aggregate *is* an account, `available balance = accounting balance − Σ active holds` is a rebuildable fold, and the hold lifecycle is exactly three engine-owned, family-agnostic cross-cutting events (`HoldPlaced → HoldCaptured | HoldExpired`). [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) fixed the authorization *technique* (synchronous, on the [ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) command surface). So this ADR is **not** where the hold lifecycle or the call regime are invented. It is where the conta à ordem becomes the **first non-degenerate Account** — the first family to actually carry the two-balance split and a hold ledger — and where the engine commits the pieces those upstream ADRs **deferred to this family**: the **hold-reconciliation policy**, ***descoberto autorizado* as a pack rule**, the **account lifecycle**, the **authorize-command contract**, and the **no-shadow-balance / v4-migration reconciliation**.

### A reconciliation the reader must see up front — the hold-event naming drift

The bd issue this resolves (`babelstone-xvcx`, filed 2026-06-20) was written **before** [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)/[033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)/[034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) landed, and its description names the hold model as **family-owned, Portuguese-named events** — `MontanteCativado → CativoCapturado → CativoExpirado`. [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) (Accepted 2026-06-23) **supersedes that framing**: a hold is recorded by the **engine-declared, family-agnostic** cross-cutting events `operations.HoldPlaced / HoldCaptured / HoldExpired` (the `FundsHeld` family in `Babelstone.Engine.CrossCuttingEvents`), carrying an opaque `account_ref`, generic `Money`, a `hold_id`, and **no family-typed shape and no PII** — and it *explicitly rejects* "holds as family events" (a per-family `DepositFundsHeld` / `AccountFundsHeld` would re-derive the cross-cutting fact per family and collide on the simple-name-keyed codec, the same failure [ADR-PC-004 A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) hit with erasure). This ADR therefore honours the **intent** of `babelstone-xvcx` (cover the hold model for the conta à ordem) by **instantiating** [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s lifecycle — it does **not** reintroduce the family-named `Cativo*` events the issue text predates and [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) forbids. This is an explicit-drift acknowledgment ([ADR-PC-020 §D3](./ADR-PC-020-llm-toolchain-and-conformance-governance.md)): the divergence is from a stale *issue description*, **toward** the Accepted ADR — the conformant direction. (*Cativo* survives as the Portuguese product term in prose and in the read surface, not as an engine event name.)

### What is genuinely new vs. the prior families

A term deposit and a personal loan are **degenerate accounts** ([ADR-PC-033 slot 1](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)): one balance, no holds. The conta à ordem is the first family that exercises the *full* Account abstraction. Six dimensions are genuinely new, and they are the whole content of the modelling:

This entry is the [ADR-PC-000 §D3](./ADR-PC-000-namespace-and-contract-shape-framework.md) **residual category** ("operational/engineering discipline … fits neither template cleanly … default to tool-selection"), the same class as [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md), [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md), and [ADR-PC-031](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md): it scopes a **family**, not a tool. The honest consequence, surfaced up front: **F1 and F2 do not discriminate** — authoring a family buys no licence, carries no PII on the durable bus ([ADR-PC-004](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)), and does not itself make the engine a PSD2 PSP ([ADR-PC-030 §F2](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md): owning the balance and the funds-and-rules stage of authorization is a ledger/decision role, not payment execution). The load-bearing question is **how to model the transactional account on the existing spine, instantiating the decided Account abstraction without re-deriving or contradicting it, and filling exactly the slots the upstream ADRs deferred here** — settled on S1–S4 plus the topology-completion reason.

### Hard filter results

#### F1 · Cost / licensing

| Candidate | Licence / cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A · model on the spine | No tool, no licence; new family projects only (the Account abstraction is already in the spine). | **Pass** |
| B · bespoke engine | Same licence (zero), but a second runtime to build and operate. | **Pass** |

| D · stored mutable balances | Same; a cache/column tier, no licence. | **Pass** |

Uniform pass — F1 does not discriminate (a family buys nothing).

#### F2 · Regulatory fit (GDPR / DORA / PSD2)

A family is not itself a regulated runtime artefact, so F2 cannot *fail* a candidate. The regulatory-weight properties this family exercises are owned by *other* ADRs and hold identically under A–D: **no PII on the durable bus** ([ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) — every account event and the `Hold` carry an opaque `account_ref` + computed `Money`, never a holder's name/NIF/IBAN), **opening stays upstream** ([ADR-PC-024](./ADR-PC-024-constitution-precondition-contract.md) / [ADR-PC-030 §P1](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)), **GDPR Article 17 crypto-shredding** via the shared erasure seam ([ADR-PC-004 §P3/A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)), and — the conta-à-ordem-specific clarification — **owning the balance and answering the funds-and-rules stage of authorization does not make the engine a PSD2 PSP** ([ADR-PC-030 §F2](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)): it never executes a payment on the rails, performs SCA, or holds card/scheme credentials (stages 1–2, 6–7 stay external).

| Candidate | GDPR | DORA / PSD2 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A / B / C / D | No PII on the bus; erasure seam present; opening upstream. | Ledger/decision component, not a PSP (stops at the wire, [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)). | **Pass** |

All clear the hard filters. The decision is entirely in S1–S4 and the topology-completion reason — the expected shape for the [§D3](./ADR-PC-000-namespace-and-contract-shape-framework.md) residual category.

### Soft criteria

#### A · Model on the spine; instantiate the Account abstraction — **CHOSEN**

**S1 · Operational complexity for 1–2 people.** Lowest available. The family is a self-contained subtree (`families/current-account/`) added the [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) way — drop in the fold project + the decider project, wired by the existing `FamilyModuleLoader` / `HostModuleLoader` assembly scans with **no per-family hand-edit** ([ADR-PC-031 §P5](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md)). Because [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) already fixed the Account abstraction and [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) already fixed the call regime, this family **adds no generic-engine type** — it *declares* its state an account through the spine seam and *implements* the family-specific policy. B is a second runtime; C and D each fight a decided abstraction (more work *and* a contradiction).

**S2 · Ecosystem coherence — decisive.** The engine commits to a **family-as-plugin** model ([ADR-PC-021 §D2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)) and to the **Account-as-fold** discipline ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)). A extends both, unchanged: the dependency arrow stays **family → engine** (gated by `ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`), the folds stay pure (the authorize decision and the reconciliation policy run command-side, gated by the determinism analysers), `available balance` stays a fold ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)), the authorize verdict is the synchronous [ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) append ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)), and the `Money` rounding discipline ([ADR-PC-010 §P1–§P2](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)) governs any fee/interest math. B bends the arrow back; **C contradicts [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s cross-cutting-hold decision**; **D contradicts the no-stored-balance rule** and the [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) lock-free guarantee.

**S3 · Exit cost.** Low. The authorize path is the [ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md)/[ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) synchronous surface, transport-independent by construction ([ADR-PC-034 §S3](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)) — the async scale-up path stays open without rework. The Account fold and the hold lifecycle are the engine's; if the family is ever retired, the cross-cutting hold events and the abstraction remain. A under-commits nothing and bakes in no transactional-account machinery the spine does not already own.

**S4 · Longevity.** Positive — this family *completes* the reference topology ([ADR-PC-030 §Consequences](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) item 4) and is the **shared shape the `credit_card` account slice will reuse** — that slice is now built **after** this family ([ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) amended 2026-06-28): the card account is the *other* instance of the same transactional-account abstraction (holds, available balance, statement cycle), so authoring the conta à ordem first builds the reusable transactional-account asset.

**Decisive project-specific reason — it completes the topology and proves the abstraction non-degenerate.** [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s thesis is one deterministic-ledger kernel expressing **every** retail product shape. With term_deposit (liability) and personal_loan (closed-end asset) built, and the credit-card slice (revolving asset) scoped, the conta à ordem is the **fourth shape and the hub the other three settle against** — and the **first to actually carry the two-balance split and a hold ledger**. Until a real transactional account lands, [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s Account abstraction is proven only on degenerate accounts; this family is the evidence that the *same* kernel folds an available balance net of holds and answers real-time authorization — the strongest demonstration the reference engine can make.

#### B · Bespoke transactional-account engine — **rejected**

A second runtime re-invents the spine two families already proved and breaks the one-engine-many-families thesis ([01 §1](../01-product-architecture.md)). Rejected on S1 + S2 with no offsetting gain.

#### C · Holds as family-owned events (the `Cativo*` framing) — **rejected (contradicts [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md))**

A per-family `MontanteCativado` / `CativoCapturado` / `CativoExpirado` re-derives the cross-cutting hold fact per family, collides on the simple-name-keyed codec, and pushes a family-typed shape into what [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) decided is a **family-agnostic** spine event ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) "Rejected: holds as family events"; [ADR-PC-021 §P2/§A5](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) forbids a family-typed spine shape). Rejected on S2 and as an explicit-drift violation of an Accepted ADR. The *intent* it encodes — that the conta à ordem has holds — is satisfied by **instantiating** [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s `operations.Hold*` lifecycle (§D2).

#### D · Stored mutable `available balance` / holds — **rejected (contradicts [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)/[034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md))**

Caching either balance as a column re-introduces a value the log and the projection can disagree about — the double-counting / shadow-balance failure mode [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) and [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) forbid — and answering a funds check from a cached balance reopens the double-spend window [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) candidate C was rejected for. Both balances stay folds; a snapshot ([ADR-PC-003](./ADR-PC-003-postgresql-snapshots.md)) is a *recomputable* optimisation, never the source of truth. Rejected on S2 + correctness.

**Decisive reason for A:** the engine is the reusable asset, the team is 1–2 people, the fold layer already commits to a `family → engine` plugin arrow, the Account abstraction and the call regime are *already decided*, and the conta à ordem completes the reference topology — all point to modelling it on the proven spine as the first instance of [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md). B/C/D each break one of those.

## Decision

### `current_account` (conta à ordem) is a transactional-account family on the [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) spine and the **first non-degenerate instance** of the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) Account abstraction; it owns the hold-reconciliation policy, *descoberto autorizado* as a pack rule, the demand-account lifecycle, and the authorize-command contract — while the hold lifecycle, the available-balance fold, and the call regime stay the upstream ADRs', unchanged.

> **Family name.** The family identifier is **`current_account`** (English snake_case, consistent with `term_deposit` and `personal_loan`); *conta à ordem* is the Portuguese product name used in prose and on the read surface. The projects are `families/current-account/`, `Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount[.Application]`; the schema version is `current_account@2026.1`.

- **D1 — A family on the proven spine.** `current_account` is authored exactly as the term-deposit and personal-loan reference families ([ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md), [ADR-PC-031 §D1](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md)): pure fold handlers + event records (`Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount`), a `LifecycleTransitions` legality table, projections, an `IFamilyModule`, and a family-owned decider (`Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount.Application`). The dependency arrow is **family → engine**; adding it is **zero generic-engine diff** — the Account abstraction, the `Movement` atom, the cross-cutting hold events, and the synchronous command surface are *already in the spine* ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)/[032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)/[034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)), so this family introduces no family-typed engine shape (gated by `ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`).

- **D2 — The first non-degenerate Account; the hold lifecycle is [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s, not re-decided.** `AccountPosition` declares the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) `IAccount`/`IHoldable` seam: it folds the **accounting balance** (`Σ Credit − Σ Debit` over its [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) `Movement`s) **and** the **available balance** (`accounting − Σ active holds [+ authorized_overdraft_limit]`, §D5). A hold's lifecycle is the engine-owned cross-cutting `operations.HoldPlaced → HoldCaptured | HoldExpired` ([ADR-PC-033 slot 2](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)), keyed by `hold_id` ([ADR-PC-033 slot 4](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)) — **this family appends and folds those events, it does not define new ones, and it does not use the `babelstone-xvcx` `Cativo*` family-event framing** (§Context reconciliation). Both balances are **folds, never stored mutable numbers**.

- **D3 — The demand-account lifecycle.** The lifecycle is `open → active → dormant → closed`, plus the open-refusal and GDPR-erasure terminals. The **family-owned events** are `AccountOpened`, `AccountOpeningFailed`, `AccountMarkedDormant`, `AccountReactivated`, and `AccountClosed`; the states are `Pending → Active → (Failed | Closed) ; Dormant ⇄ Active ; Erased`. GDPR erasure is **not** a family-owned event — the terminal `Erased` transition is driven by the engine-declared cross-cutting `operations.PersonalDataErasureRequested` folded via the `IErasable` seam ([ADR-PC-004 §P3/A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md), [ADR-PC-031 §D2](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md)). The single `LifecycleTransitions` table is the auditable legality source the decider consults before appending; every business-terminal state is closed to every business transition; `Dormant` is a non-terminal reversible state (a dormant account reactivates on customer-initiated movement). **The everyday credit/debit postings** (salary in, bills out, the deposit/loan/card legs that settle against the account) arrive as [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) `Movement`s — `Originated` for engine-initiated legs, `Observed` for the cleared/captured posting feed — whose **wire contract (shape, ordering, idempotency) is a future contract-shape ADR** ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md): "the settlement/posting feed … a future contract-shape ADR"); this family folds them into the balance, it does not fix that feed's wire here.

- **D4 — The hold-reconciliation policy (the family content [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) deferred).** Within [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s three transitions (this is **policy, not new transitions**):
  - **Partial capture** — a `HoldCaptured` for **less** than the held amount releases the remainder: the captured `Movement` moves the accounting balance, the residual leaves the active set (restoring that slice of available balance). No new event; the partial amount rides the `HoldCaptured`.
  - **Late capture vs expiry** — a capture that arrives **after** the matching `HoldExpired` is a **re-presentment**: it is recorded as an `Observed` posting `Movement` against the accounting balance (the money genuinely cleared) but **does not resurrect the expired hold**, and it is **surfaced as a reconciliation signal** (the available balance was already restored at expiry; a post-expiry capture is a correctness event ops must see), never silently absorbed.
  - **Over-capture** — a `HoldCaptured` for **more** than the held amount posts the captured `Movement` but raises a reconciliation signal (the decider never *places* a hold over available balance, §D6; an over-capture means the cleared amount exceeded the earmark — a feed/merchant anomaly).
  - **Reversal** — an authorization voided before capture is a **`HoldExpired`-style release with no posting** (no money moved), distinct from a capture; it is appended off the reversal signal, not a timer.
  - **The expiry horizon** — a hold's time-to-live is a **product/pack value** (per [ADR-PC-006](./ADR-PC-006-cue-schema-language.md)/[ADR-PC-007](./ADR-PC-007-signed-yaml-oci-pack.md), alongside the limits of §D5). `HoldExpired` is appended by an operator/command-shell action reading a **projection over the active-hold set against a value-date horizon** — **never a clock-manufactured engine event** ([ADR-PC-023](./ADR-PC-023-temporal-signals-projection-derived.md), [ADR-PC-033 slot 2](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)). This keeps the fold pure and replay deterministic.

- **D5 — *Descoberto autorizado* is a pack rule; *ultrapassagem* is refused.** An **arranged overdraft** is a **declarative pack limit** ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md): "overdraft and limits are pack rules") evaluated at **authorization stage 4**: it extends the available-balance identity to `available balance = accounting balance − Σ active holds + authorized_overdraft_limit`, so a debit within the arranged limit is authorized and a debit beyond it (an **`ultrapassagem` / *descoberto não autorizado***) is **refused** — a `declined` verdict with reason `OVERDRAFT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (§D6), never a silently-allowed overshoot. Transaction/velocity limits are expressed in the same pack surface and evaluated at the same stage. The decider **reads** the pack limit; the fold stays pure. Interest/fees on a used *descoberto* are family product-math (an accrual against the negative balance, the [ADR-PC-031 §P2](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md) `FinancialMath` discipline) — modelled when the family is built, not detailed here. **The [ADR-PC-006](./ADR-PC-006-cue-schema-language.md)/[ADR-PC-007](./ADR-PC-007-signed-yaml-oci-pack.md) CUE grammar expansion that expresses the limit/overdraft constructs is a named follow-up** ([ADR-PC-033 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md): "pack-grammar expansion … is a separate change") — this ADR fixes the *model* (descoberto as a pack-read limit term in the available-balance fold), the grammar lands with the family.

- **D6 — The authorize-command contract (delegated here by [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)).** The external authorization pipeline ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) stages 1–2, 6–7) calls the engine's **synchronous, idempotent** command surface ([ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md), [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)) to answer stages 3–5:
  - **Request** — `account_ref` (opaque, never PII — [ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)), `amount` (`Money`, integer cents — [ADR-PC-010 §P2](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)), `value_date`, and the command id / `Idempotency-Key` ([ADR-PC-029 slot 4](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md)).
  - **Response** — `authorized` (with the `hold_id` of the appended `HoldPlaced`) **or** `declined` with a **reason taxonomy**: `INSUFFICIENT_AVAILABLE_BALANCE`, `OVERDRAFT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (a transaction/velocity pack limit), and `ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE` (dormant / closed / blocked — the lifecycle gate). The decline is **terminal and gated** — the caller must honour it ([ADR-PC-033 slot 5](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)).
  - **Decide** — a pure read-state-and-append: fold available balance, apply pack rules (limits, *descoberto* §D5), append `HoldPlaced` (`authorized`) **or** append nothing and return `declined`. No clock, no external call ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)).
  - **Idempotent + de-settled** — a replayed command id returns the **original** verdict (the same `hold_id` / `commit_sequence`) with **no second `HoldPlaced`** ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) `AUTHORIZATION_SYNC_IDEMPOTENT`, whose **test this family writes**); the authorize append **never moves money** — capture/settlement is the separate gated leg, arriving as `HoldCaptured` ([ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) `Observed`). The concrete authorize-command **wire shape** (and any bus-promoted hold-event `.avsc`) is the **contract-reviewer**'s lane, authored with the family ([ADR-PC-033 slot 1](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)).

- **D7 — Opening is upstream; the no-shadow-balance / v4-migration reconciliation is explicit.** Account **opening** arrives **already approved** (KYC/AML, eligibility upstream — [ADR-PC-024](./ADR-PC-024-constitution-precondition-contract.md), [ADR-PC-030 §P1](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)); the engine records `AccountOpened`, it never adjudicates the opening. The **no-shadow-balance rule is coexistence-topology-scoped**, not a permanent prohibition. Its source, [02 §3 commitment 1](../02-v1-scope-term-deposits.md), reads verbatim:
  > "**The current account is read through, not owned.** … The engine does not maintain a shadow balance — that would be the double-counting failure mode."

  Through **v1–v3** the legacy core authoritatively owns the conta à ordem balance and the engine holds **no shadow balance** ([ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) unchanged; settlement flows through the five-command adapter, not an engine-owned balance). At **v4** the conta à ordem **migrates onto the engine** *as an instance of this family* — and the engine **being the authoritative owner** is the **opposite** of a shadow, so there is no contradiction ([ADR-PC-030 §Decision item 4](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md): "*The engine being the authoritative owner at v4 is the opposite of a shadow*"). The v4 cutover follows the **SoR-transition / book-migration model** of [coexistence §9](../feature-design-strangler-fig-coexistence.md) — the correlation-linked SoR-transition event chain whose term-deposit form is "renewal of a legacy in-flight deposit creates a new engine instance" (the migration-by-renewal model); the conta à ordem migration is an SoR transition of the **same shape** (a `LegacyInstanceObserved`-linked transfer of authority), its **concrete mechanism a v4-owned design, not fixed here**. The **authorization boundary** is [ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s: the engine owns **stages 3–5** (funds, pack rules, the hold); **SCA (stage 2), fraud (stage 6), and the rails/scheme/clearing/settlement (stages 1, 7) stay external**. The real-time **technique is no longer open** — it is [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)'s **synchronous** call (the `babelstone-xvcx` "real-time technique left to its separate issue" is **discharged**: that issue, `babelstone-mz33`, is closed and decided in [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)).

**Rejected: a bespoke transactional-account engine** — re-invents the spine, breaks one-engine-many-families. **Rejected: holds as family-owned `Cativo*` events** — contradicts [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s cross-cutting decision. **Rejected: stored mutable balances** — contradicts the no-stored-balance / no-shadow-balance rule and reopens the double-spend window.

## Implementation Principles

### P1 — Project topology (the [ADR-PC-021 §P1](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) / [ADR-PC-031 §P1](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md) shape)

```
families/current-account/src/
  Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount/             pure folds + events + projections + lifecycle table
      refs: Babelstone.Engine, Babelstone.FinancialTypes        (cannot reach a DB or the kernel)
      — declares AccountPosition : IAccount, IHoldable (the ADR-PC-033 seam)
  Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount.Application/  the decider (commands → events) incl. the authorize path
      refs: Babelstone.Engine, Babelstone.Engine.Hosting, Babelstone.FinancialMath,
            Babelstone.FinancialTypes, Babelstone.RateSheets, Babelstone.Packs,
            Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount
contracts/
  avro/accounts/current_account/*.avsc            the bus-published event payload schemas (store-only events absent)
  cue/families/current-account.cue                the product-config family schema (incl. the descoberto/limit constructs, §D5)
```

The Account abstraction's generic half — the `IAccount`/`IHoldable` interfaces, the generic balance/available-balance/active-hold projector, the `operations.HoldPlaced/HoldCaptured/HoldExpired` records + Avro, and the authorization decider scaffold — is the **engine half of bd `babelstone-hkwp.3`** ([ADR-PC-033 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)), a tracked follow-up this family builds against; it is **not** authored in `families/**`.

### P2 — `AccountPosition` is the first state to declare the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) seam

`AccountPosition` implements `IAccount` (it folds an accounting balance over `Movement`s) **and** `IHoldable` (it folds the active-hold set, so the spine's generic projector derives `available balance = accounting − Σ active holds [+ authorized_overdraft_limit]`). term_deposit's `DepositPosition` and personal_loan's `LoanPosition` remain degenerate accounts (`IAccount`, no `IHoldable`); this family is the first `IHoldable`. No family-typed shape enters the spine — the seam is an interface a family implements on its *own* state ([ADR-PC-033 slot 6](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)).

### P3 — Folds stay pure; the authorize decision and the reconciliation policy are command-side

The folds LABEL state and accumulate carried facts (the posted `Movement`s, the active holds, the lifecycle state) — they never read a clock, do I/O, decide an authorization, or compute an expiry. The **decider** reads the available-balance fold and the pack limits and appends `HoldPlaced`-or-refusal (§D6); the **hold-reconciliation policy** (§D4) and the **expiry-horizon projection read** (§D4, [ADR-PC-023](./ADR-PC-023-temporal-signals-projection-derived.md)) are command-shell/operator actions over projections. The folds record the resulting facts. This is the [ADR-PC-021 §D3](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) / [ADR-PC-031 §P3](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md) purity discipline, gated by the determinism analysers.

### P4 — *Descoberto* and limits are a pack read at stage 4; the available-balance identity carries the overdraft term

The decider evaluates the pack's `authorized_overdraft_limit` and any transaction/velocity limits at authorization stage 4 (§D5), so the **same pack surface that prices the product also governs "can this debit go through."** The available-balance fold ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)) is read net of holds **plus** the arranged overdraft headroom. The CUE constructs for the limit/overdraft are the named pack-grammar follow-up (§D5).

### P5 — Discovery is host assembly-scan; no per-family hand-edit

The family is discovered by the existing `FamilyModuleLoader` (folds) and `HostModuleLoader` (host wiring) assembly scans ([ADR-PC-021 §A13–§A14](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md), [ADR-PC-031 §P5](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md)). Authoring the family is its projects + the host `ProjectReference` (the scan anchor), never a surgical edit to generic compose code.

### P6 — No PII; the shared erasure seam

Every account event and the `Hold` carry an opaque `account_ref` and computed `Money`, never a holder's name/NIF/IBAN, in cleartext or ciphertext ([ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)). GDPR erasure rides the one cross-cutting `operations.PersonalDataErasureRequested` folded via `IErasable` (§D3) — declared once in the spine, not re-owned here.

## Consequences

**What this choice makes easier:**

1. **The reference topology is complete.** Four product shapes on one kernel — liability, closed-end asset, revolving asset, and now the transactional account / hub ([ADR-PC-030 §Consequences](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) item 4) — the strongest demonstration the family abstraction generalises across the whole retail topology.
2. **The Account abstraction is proven non-degenerate.** [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s two-balance/hold model gets its first real instance: the same kernel folds an available balance net of holds and answers real-time authorization, lock-free ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)).
3. **A reusable transactional-account shape for the card slice.** The `credit_card` account slice (built **after** this family per the [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) 2026-06-28 amendment) is the *other* instance of this abstraction; it inherits the hold/available-balance/authorize machinery rather than re-deriving it.
4. **The authorize contract is concrete.** [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)'s delegated payload now has a shape, a `declined` taxonomy, and a home for the `AUTHORIZATION_SYNC_IDEMPOTENT` test.
5. **The v4 migration has a named destination and a reconciliation that holds.** [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md)'s "moves onto the engine at v4" is this family; the no-shadow-balance rule is shown to be coexistence-topology-scoped, not violated (§D7).

**What this choice makes harder or locks in:**

1. **The engine is on the payment hot path for this family.** Its latency and availability become payment-path SLOs ([ADR-PC-034 §Consequences](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md), [ADR-PC-001](./ADR-PC-001-event-store-technology.md)/[ADR-PC-011](./ADR-PC-011-in-house-load-test-harness.md) load-bearing). This is [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)'s consequence, inherited, not newly chosen here.
2. **Hold reconciliation is correctness-sensitive.** Partial/late/over-capture and reversal (§D4) are a reconciliation surface this family must own and test; the fold trusts its stream, the decider prevents over-earmarking, and the gap between them is a monitored signal ([ADR-PC-033 §Consequences](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)).
3. **The pack surface widens.** Expressing *descoberto*/limits as pack rules grows the [ADR-PC-006](./ADR-PC-006-cue-schema-language.md)/[ADR-PC-007](./ADR-PC-007-signed-yaml-oci-pack.md) grammar; it must stay declarative ([ADR-PC-030 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)).

**Residual risks (what this ADR does *not* commit to):**

- **The Account-abstraction engine implementation is not built here.** The `IAccount`/`IHoldable` interfaces, the generic balance/available-balance/active-hold projector, the concrete `operations.Hold*` records + Avro, and the authorization decider scaffold are the **engine half of bd `babelstone-hkwp.3`** ([ADR-PC-033 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)). This family builds *against* them; it does not author the spine half.
- **The everyday posting feed's wire contract is a future contract-shape ADR** ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)). This ADR fixes that postings *are* `Movement`s folded into the balance; it does not fix the feed's shape/ordering/idempotency.
- **The *descoberto*/limit CUE grammar is a named follow-up** (§D5, [ADR-PC-033 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)).
- **Statement issuance is out of scope** — an issued statement is a **sealed event**, not a replayable projection ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md), [ADR-PC-033 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)); it is a separate family-owned design.
- **The v4 migration mechanism is v4-owned.** §D7 fixes the *reconciliation* (no-shadow → engine-owned is not a contradiction) and the *pattern* ([coexistence §9](../feature-design-strangler-fig-coexistence.md) SoR transition); the concrete cutover/back-loading design is a v4 change, not this ADR.
- **Authorization-path latency/availability SLOs and the *descoberto* interest math are set when the family is built** ([ADR-PC-034 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)).
