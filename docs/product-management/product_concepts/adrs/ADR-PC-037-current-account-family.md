## Context

[ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) fixed what babelstone is *for* — a core product & account ledger — and drew a **family roadmap** spanning the retail product topology: a liability (`term_deposit`, *built*), a **closed-end asset** (`personal_loan`, *built* — [ADR-PC-031](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md)), an **open-end revolving asset** (`credit_card`, [ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md)), and the **transactional/demand account** (`conta à ordem`) — the hub the other three settle against. [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) then fixed the **Account abstraction** every engine-owned aggregate is: a balance is a fold over `Movement`s, and a **transactional** account additionally folds a hold ledger so `available balance = accounting balance − Σ active holds`. That ADR called the `conta à ordem` "the hardest family" and named it the abstraction's **first transactional instance** — the family this ADR now scopes.

**In plain English.** A *conta à ordem* — a current or demand account — is the everyday bank account: salary lands in it, direct debits and card legs come out of it, and at any instant it has two subtly different balances. The **accounting balance** is what has actually posted; the **available balance** is what you can spend *right now*, which is lower whenever some money is earmarked by an approved-but-not-yet-settled payment (the classic case is a hotel or petrol pre-authorization). That earmark is a **hold**, and a hold has a tiny lifecycle of its own — it is *placed* when a payment is authorized, *captured* when the settlement finally arrives, or *expires* if it times out. This family is the **first** in babelstone to carry that real two-balance split and a hold ledger; the deposit and loan families are "degenerate" accounts — one balance, no holds — so they proved nothing about the transactional shape. The heavy machinery is **already decided**: the Account abstraction and its holds ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)), the `Movement` read side ([ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)), and the synchronous authorization *technique* ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)) all landed under the shape-3 foundations. This ADR is the **family build on top of that foundation**: it makes the account real (`AccountPosition` declares itself an account through the `IAccount`/`IHoldable` seam), answers the real-time "can this debit go through?" question (the **authorize** command, with a **declined** reason taxonomy), specifies exactly how holds *reconcile* when a capture is partial, late, or too big, and turns the arranged overdraft (*descoberto autorizado*) into a **pack rule** the authorization decider reads. The everyday stream of already-cleared money movements that feeds the account (salary in, bills out, captured card legs) arrives from outside the engine as `Observed` `Movement`s; the **wire contract** for that feed is a sibling contract-shape ADR this family *consumes* (bd `babelstone-30hf.5`), not one it restates.

This entry is the [ADR-PC-000 §D3](./ADR-PC-000-namespace-and-contract-shape-framework.md) **residual category** ("operational/engineering discipline … fits neither template cleanly … default to tool-selection"), the same class as [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md), [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md), [ADR-PC-031](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md), and [ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md): it scopes a **family**, not a tool. The honest consequence, surfaced up front: **F1 and F2 do not discriminate** — authoring a family buys no licence, and a current-account family carries **no PII on the durable bus** (the no-PII posture every family holds, [ADR-PC-004](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) — `account_ref` is opaque, never a name/NIF/IBAN) and is **not itself a regulated runtime artefact**. The load-bearing question is **how to model the first genuinely-transactional account — the two-balance split, the hold ledger and its reconciliation policy, the real-time authorize decision, and the arranged overdraft — entirely on the existing spine, as the *first instance* of the fixed [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) abstraction rather than a re-derivation of it, with zero generic-engine diff and the clearing/settlement/scheme machinery firmly outside**.

### Build order and reuse — the current account is built first, the card follows it

Per [ADR-PC-030 §P2 as revised 2026-07-02](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md), the roadmap order for the two transactional families is `conta à ordem` **before** `credit_card`. The reason is reuse, not preference. The `conta à ordem` is where the holds, the `available balance` fold, and the real-time authorize path are first *implemented* against a live family; the card account ([ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md)) is the **second** instance of that exact shape and lands against a *proven* implementation instead of co-inventing it. This ADR therefore carries the weight of proving the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) abstraction generalises to a real transactional account — the same de-risking move [ADR-PC-031](./ADR-PC-031-personal-loan-family.md) made (the loan reused the deposit shape) and [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) made (fixing the Account model before its first transactional family).

### What is genuinely new vs. the term deposit and the closed-end loan

The current account reuses the [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) plugin spine wholesale, the [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) `Movement` substrate wholesale, and the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) Account contract wholesale. Four dimensions are genuinely new — none of them exercised by the degenerate deposit/loan accounts — and they are the whole content of the modelling:

| # | New dimension | What it is | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The accounting/available split + the hold ledger** (*first non-degenerate account*) | The account carries two balances whose gap is the held amount: `available balance = accounting balance − Σ active holds [+ arranged overdraft]`. Neither is a stored number; both are folds ([ADR-PC-033 slot 1](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)). This is the first family to fold a non-empty hold set. | `AccountPosition` declares the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) `IAccount` + `IHoldable` seam; the **spine-owned** generic projector derives both balances — the family supplies only its state. |
| 2 | **The real-time authorize decision + the declined taxonomy** | The funds-and-rules core of authorization ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) stages 3–5): fold `available balance`, apply pack rules (limits, overdraft), and append `HoldPlaced` (`authorized`) **or** a refusal fact (`declined`, with a bounded reason taxonomy). | The family-owned decider on the [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) synchronous idempotent path ([ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) surface, heavier load profile). |
| 3 | **The hold-reconciliation policy** | Holds do not always resolve cleanly — a capture can be *partial*, arrive *after* the hold expired, be *larger* than the hold, or be *reversed*, and a hold *times out*. | 4 | **arranged overdraft** as a pack rule | An agreed overdraft lets the account go below zero up to a limit; a debit within the limit is authorized, one beyond it (unarranged overdraft) is refused. The limit is a **declarative pack rule** the authorize decider reads at stage 4, extending the available identity by `+ arranged_overdraft_limit`; a used overdraft accrues overdraft interest/fees. | The [ADR-PC-006](./ADR-PC-006-cue-schema-language.md)/[ADR-PC-007](./ADR-PC-007-signed-yaml-oci-pack.md) pack grammar (a declarative limit construct); the accrual is `Money`-disciplined math run command-side, modelled like the loan's accrual. |

## Evaluation

### Hard filter results

#### F1 · Cost / licensing

| Candidate | Licence / cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A · current account on the Account abstraction | No tool, no licence; new family projects + a pack-grammar expansion + an overdraft-accrual addition to the existing `Babelstone.FinancialMath`. | **Pass** |
| B · bespoke current-account engine | Same licence (zero), but a second runtime to build and operate. | **Pass** |
| C · stored available-balance cache | Same; no new project (a column). | **Pass** |
| D · in-family clearing/settlement | Same; but a settlement/clearing surface a 1–2-person team should not own. | **Pass** |

Uniform pass — F1 does not discriminate (a family buys nothing).

#### F2 · Regulatory fit (GDPR / DORA / PSD2)

A family is not itself a regulated runtime artefact, so F2 cannot *fail* a candidate. The regulatory-weight properties this family exercises are owned by *other* ADRs and hold identically under candidates A/C: **no PII on the durable bus** ([ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) — every event carries computed facts + an opaque `account_ref`, never a name/NIF/IBAN), **account opening stays upstream** ([ADR-PC-024](./ADR-PC-024-constitution-precondition-contract.md) / [ADR-PC-030 §P1](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) — KYC/onboarding is upstream; the engine records the verdict, never runs it), **GDPR erasure** rides the shared `IErasable` seam ([ADR-PC-004 §A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)), and **the engine is not a PSD2 payment-services provider** ([ADR-PC-030 §F2](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) — it never executes on the rails, performs SCA, screens fraud, clears or settles). The PT/EU overdraft (*descoberto*) rate/fee caps and consumer-protection limits are *correctness* properties of the decider's math and the pack constraints, gated by tests, not a filter a candidate passes or fails.

| Candidate | GDPR | DORA / PSD2 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A / C | No PII on the bus; erasure event present; opening upstream. | No payment execution, no rails/scheme/SCA/fraud/clearing/settlement in-engine (stops at the wire, [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)); the engine is a real-time authorization dependency — an availability concern, not a regulated activity ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)). | **Pass** |
| D · in-family clearing/settlement | Largest surface — cleared-payment matching at volume. | Family performs clearing/settlement matching — payment execution the boundary excludes. | **Pass** (largest surface; contradicts [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)) |

A and C clear the hard filters; C **contradicts the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) no-stored-balance rule** by construction, and D **contradicts the [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) boundary** by construction. The decision is in S1–S4 plus the account-fold and boundary analysis — the expected shape for the [§D3](./ADR-PC-000-namespace-and-contract-shape-framework.md) residual category.

### Soft criteria

#### A · Current account as the first transactional instance of the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) Account — **CHOSEN**

**S1 · Operational complexity for 1–2 people.** Lowest of the account-modelling options. The family is a self-contained subtree (`families/current-account/`) added the [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) way — drop in the fold project + the decider project, wire nothing in the generic engine. The two-balance split, the `HoldPlaced → HoldCaptured | HoldExpired` lifecycle, the generic balance/available/active-hold projector, and the synchronous idempotent authorize surface are **already provided by the spine** (the engine half of bd `babelstone-hkwp.3`, [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) + [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)); the family supplies only its own state through the `IAccount`/`IHoldable` seam, its lifecycle events, its reconciliation policy, and its pack constructs. The one generic-side touch is additive: an overdraft-accrual routine beside the existing `Accrual`/`Amortization` kernels in `Babelstone.FinancialMath`, which names no family.

**S2 · Ecosystem coherence — decisive.** The engine already commits to a **family-as-plugin** model ([ADR-PC-021 §D2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)) and a **transactional-account abstraction** ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)); A is the abstraction's *first instance* — it makes the fixed contract real without a new spine concept. The dependency arrow stays **family → engine** (gated by `ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`), the folds stay pure (the authorize math and the overdraft accrual run command-side, gated by the determinism analysers), both balances stay folds (no stored column, [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)), and holds are the cross-cutting `operations.Hold*` records the spine owns — no per-family `AccountFundsHeld`. B bends the arrow back (a second runtime); C breaks the account-is-a-fold rule; D drags clearing/settlement across the boundary.

**S3 · Exit cost.** Low. The authorize/hold path *is* the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)/[ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) path; the posting feed is a *consumed* contract this family owns as a sibling ADR (bd `babelstone-30hf.5`), so the account carries no clearing/settlement code to unwind. The overdraft accrual sits beside `Accrual`/`Amortization` in `FinancialMath`. Nothing family-specific leaks into the generic engine, so retiring or reshaping the family is a subtree deletion.

**S4 · Longevity.** Positive — this family is the reference transactional account every later transactional family inherits. The card account ([ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md)) already reuses its holds, available-balance fold, and authorize path; a future savings-with-overdraft or store-card family inherits the same, plus the reconciliation policy and the overdraft pack constructs. Proving the abstraction here is what makes those cheap.

**Decisive project-specific reason — proves the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) abstraction generalises to a *real* transactional account, and delivers the hub the topology settles against.** [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s thesis is one deterministic-ledger kernel expressing every retail product shape. The demand account is the **hub** — the shape the other three settle against — and modelling it as the *first instance* of the fixed Account contract (two balances as folds, a hold ledger, a real-time authorize decider) is the strongest evidence the abstraction is not secretly deposit-shaped. A delivers that with zero generic-engine diff, holds cross-cutting, and the clearing/settlement machinery outside.

#### B · Bespoke current-account engine — **rejected**

A second runtime re-invents the spine the deposit/loan families already proved and the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) Account contract already fixed, and breaks the one-engine-many-families thesis ([01 §1](../01-product-architecture.md)). Rejected on S1 + S2 with no offsetting gain — every transactional dimension it would build is already available on the spine.

#### C · Stored, mutable available-balance column — **rejected**

Caching the available balance as a column re-introduces a value the log and the projection can disagree about — the shadow-balance / double-counting failure mode [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) and [ADR-PC-030 §95](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) forbid and [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) rejects at the contract level ("no balance is ever a stored mutable number; both balances and every hold are pure, rebuildable folds"). Answering authorize from a possibly-stale cached balance, or deferring the `HoldPlaced` append, reopens the double-spend window the synchronous-hold guarantee closes ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) candidate C, [ADR-PC-030 §48](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)). Rejected on correctness (S2). A recomputable snapshot/read-model ([ADR-PC-003](./ADR-PC-003-postgresql-snapshots.md), [ADR-PC-027](./ADR-PC-027-deposit-read-surface-canonical-resource.md)) is a *derived* optimisation, never the authorization source of truth.

#### D · In-family clearing/settlement logic — **rejected**

The everyday posting feed (salary in, bills out, captured card legs) is a stream of **already-cleared** movements that arrive from *outside* the engine. Modelling how they clear/settle inside the family pulls the rails/scheme machinery [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) holds outside back across the boundary and re-implements matching logic the family should *consume as data*. The feed is an `Observed` `Movement` contract ([ADR-PC-032 §A4](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)) whose wire shape is the settlement/posting-feed contract-shape ADR (bd `babelstone-30hf.5`) — the family declares the `reason` strings its postings carry and folds them; it never owns clearing. Rejected on S2 + the [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) boundary.

**Decisive reason for A:** the engine is the reusable asset, the transactional-account shape is already fixed ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)) and its synchronous technique already picked ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)) — so modelling the current account as the *first instance* of that contract, with holds cross-cutting, the posting feed consumed, and overdraft a pack rule, is the only candidate that adds the transactional/demand shape with zero generic-engine diff while keeping clearing/settlement outside and both balances as folds. B/C/D each break one of those.

## Decision

### `current_account` (conta à ordem) is the **first transactional instance** of the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) Account on the [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) spine: a two-balance account with a hold ledger, a synchronous idempotent authorize decider, a family-owned hold-reconciliation policy, and arranged overdraft as a pack rule — with clearing/settlement outside the boundary and both balances kept as folds.

- **D1 — A family on the proven spine.** `current_account` is authored exactly as the reference families: pure fold handlers + event records (`Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount`), a lifecycle legality table, projections, an `IFamilyModule`, and a family-owned decider (`Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount.Application`). The dependency arrow is **family → engine**; adding it is **zero generic-engine diff** — the only generic addition is an additive overdraft-accrual routine in `Babelstone.FinancialMath`, which names no family (gated by `ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`). Discovery is the existing host assembly-scan; there is no per-family hand-edit to generic compose code.
- **D2 — The transactional/demand-account lifecycle.** The account lifecycle is `open → active (transacting) → (dormant ↔ active) → closed`, plus the open-refusal and GDPR-erasure terminals. The family-owned events are `AccountOpened`, `AccountOpeningFailed`, `AccountMarkedDormant`, `AccountReactivated`, `AccountClosed`; the states are `Pending → Active → (Failed | Closed) ; Dormant ⇄ Active ; Erased`. **`Dormant` is a non-terminal, reversible state** (an inactive account reactivates on use), distinguishing this family from the loan's good-or-closed binary. The **holds** (`HoldPlaced → HoldCaptured | HoldExpired`) and the **posted movements** are **not** family-owned events — they are the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) cross-cutting `operations.Hold*` records and the [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) `Movement`s the spine already owns, *instantiated* here, not re-decided. GDPR erasure is likewise the engine-declared cross-cutting `operations.PersonalDataErasureRequested` folded via `IErasable` ([ADR-PC-004 §A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)). The single `LifecycleTransitions` table is the auditable legality source the decider consults before appending; every operating transition runs only from `Active`, and `Dormant` is legal only between `Active` states.
- **D3 — `AccountPosition` is the first non-degenerate account; it declares the [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) seam and the everyday posting feed is *consumed*, not owned.** `AccountPosition` implements the spine-owned `IAccount` **and** `IHoldable` seams: it folds the accounting balance over [ADR-PC-032](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md) `Movement`s **and** folds the active-hold set, so the **generic projector** derives `available balance = accounting balance − Σ active holds [+ arranged overdraft]`. This is the **first family** to carry a real accounting/available split and a non-empty hold ledger — the deposit and loan are degenerate accounts ([ADR-PC-033 slot 1](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)). Neither balance is a stored column; both are query-time folds. The everyday credit/debit posting feed (salary in, bills out, captured card legs) arrives as **`Observed` `Movement`s** ([ADR-PC-032 §A4](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)); its **wire contract** — payload, ordering, at-least-once + dedup, gap-detection ownership — is **deferred to the settlement/posting-feed contract-shape ADR this epic owns** (bd `babelstone-30hf.5`). This family *consumes* that contract: it declares the `reason` strings its postings carry and folds them into the accounting balance and hold releases; it never owns, restates, or hand-codes clearing/settlement.
- **D4 — The hold-reconciliation policy, within [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)'s three transitions.** [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) fixed the three transitions and their `hold_id` idempotency but deferred the *reconciliation policy* to this family. The policy is fixed as follows, all **within** those three transitions (a policy, not new transitions): (a) **partial capture** — a `HoldCaptured` for less than the held amount posts the captured `Movement` (moving the accounting balance) and releases the remainder (it leaves the active set); (b) **late capture vs expiry** — a capture arriving after the matching `HoldExpired` is a **re-presentment**: record it as an `Observed` posting `Movement` against the accounting balance, do **not** resurrect the expired hold, and raise a **reconciliation signal**; (c) **over-capture** — a `HoldCaptured` for more than the held amount posts the `Movement` and raises a reconciliation signal; (d) **reversal** — an authorization voided before capture is a `HoldExpired`-style release with **no posting** (no money moved); (e) **expiry horizon** — a product/pack time-to-live: `HoldExpired` is appended off a **projection over the active-hold set against a value-date horizon** ([ADR-PC-023](./ADR-PC-023-temporal-signals-projection-derived.md)), fired by the [ADR-PC-036](./ADR-PC-036-lifecycle-command-driver.md) lifecycle-command driver, **never a clock-manufactured engine event**. The fold stays pure and replay-deterministic throughout (gated by `HOLD_LIFECYCLE_PURE`, whose first *family* instance lands here).
- **D5 — arranged overdraft is a declarative pack rule; unarranged overdraft is refused.** The arranged overdraft is a **declarative pack limit** ([ADR-PC-006](./ADR-PC-006-cue-schema-language.md)/[ADR-PC-007](./ADR-PC-007-signed-yaml-oci-pack.md)) the authorize decider reads at **stage 4** ([ADR-PC-030 §P3](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)), extending the available-balance identity to `available = accounting − Σ active holds + arranged_overdraft_limit`. A debit **within** the arranged limit is authorized; a debit **beyond** it — an **unarranged overdraft** (*ultrapassagem*) — is **refused** (`declined: OVERDRAFT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`). The CUE pack grammar expands with the `arranged_overdraft_limit` construct **and** velocity/transaction-limit constructs — the named pack-grammar follow-up [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) and [ADR-PC-030 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) deferred; the grammar stays **declarative**. A used overdraft accrues overdraft interest/fees against the negative balance — `Money`-disciplined math run **command-side** ([ADR-PC-010 §P1–§P2](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)), modelled like the loan's accrual and conserving to the cent. The **pack owns** the limit/rate values; the decider evaluates them.
- **D6 — The authorize command is synchronous, idempotent, and gated; it appends `HoldPlaced` or a refusal fact.** The authorize command answers **synchronously** on the [ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) command surface under a heavier load profile — the [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) technique, not a new ingress. The request carries `account_ref` (opaque, no PII), `amount` (`Money`, integer cents), `value_date`, and a command id / `Idempotency-Key`. `Decide` is a **pure read-state-and-append**: fold `available balance`, apply pack rules (limits, overdraft at stage 4), and append `HoldPlaced` (response `authorized`, carrying the `hold_id`) **or append a refusal fact** and return `declined` with a bounded reason taxonomy — `INSUFFICIENT_AVAILABLE_BALANCE`, `OVERDRAFT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (velocity/transaction), `ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE` (dormant/closed/blocked). No clock, no external call. It is **idempotent**: a replayed command id returns the **original** verdict (the same `hold_id`) with **no second `HoldPlaced`** ([ADR-PC-029 slot 4](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md), `ENGINE_COMMAND_IDEMPOTENT`, on the payment hot path). It is **de-settled**: the authorize append never moves money; capture arrives later as `HoldCaptured` (D4). The refusal **fact is appended** — the decider does not merely return `declined` data; recording the refusal is the family command shell's obligation (the deferral flagged on bd `babelstone-30hf.2`), so a decline is an auditable event, not a silent non-append.

*This family also **honours** [ADR-PC-041](./ADR-PC-041-operation-constraining-legal-holds-and-freezes.md): where a legal hold or freeze is present on an account, the generic available-balance fold already subtracts active legal holds (`available = accounting − Σ active authorization-holds − Σ active legal-holds`, [ADR-PC-033 §Decision revised 2026-07-05](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)), so the authorize decider refuses a debit that a freeze forbids without this family re-deciding the legal-hold contract. It is referenced, not amended.*

**Rejected: a bespoke current-account engine** — re-invents the spine and the fixed Account contract ([ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)), breaks one-engine-many-families. **Rejected: a stored, mutable available-balance column** — the shadow-balance failure the Account contract forbids; reopens the double-spend window. **Rejected: in-family clearing/settlement** — pulls the excluded rails/scheme machinery across the [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) boundary; the posting feed is a consumed `Observed`-`Movement` contract.

## Implementation Principles

### P1 — Project topology (the [ADR-PC-021 §P1](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) shape)

```
families/current-account/src/
  Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount/              pure folds + events + projections + lifecycle table
      refs: Babelstone.Engine, Babelstone.FinancialTypes        (cannot reach a DB or the kernel)
      AccountPosition : IAccount, IHoldable                     (the ADR-PC-033 seam)
  Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount.Application/  the decider (authorize + lifecycle commands → events)
      refs: Babelstone.Engine, Babelstone.FinancialMath, Babelstone.FinancialTypes,
            Babelstone.RateSheets, Babelstone.Packs, Babelstone.Families.CurrentAccount
engine/src/Babelstone.FinancialMath/
  + overdraft accrual                             the one additive, family-agnostic routine
contracts/
  avro/accounts/current_account/*.avsc             the bus-published event payload schemas
  cue/families/current-account.cue                 the product-config family schema
                                                   (+ arranged_overdraft_limit + velocity/transaction limits)
```

### P2 — `AccountPosition` is a transactional [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) account; both balances are folds

`AccountPosition` declares itself an account through the spine-owned `IAccount` seam and a *holdable* account through `IHoldable`; the **spine-owned generic projector** derives `available balance = accounting balance − Σ active holds [+ arranged overdraft]` — the family supplies only its state, no balance is a stored mutable number ([ADR-PC-033 slot 1/3](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)). The folds **label** state and **accumulate** carried facts (the accounting balance over `Movement`s, the active-hold set, the pinned overdraft-in-use figure) — they never *compute* an authorization verdict or an overdraft accrual. A rebuild re-derives both balances and the active-hold set identically from the stream (gated by `ACCOUNT_BALANCE_IS_A_FOLD`).

### P3 — Folds stay pure; the decider is the impure layer

The authorize decision, the overdraft accrual math, and the reconciliation-signal emission run **command-side** in the decider; the folds record the already-computed facts. This is the same fold-purity discipline the deposit/loan families hold — no clock, no I/O, no randomness in a fold (gated by `DETERMINISM_GATE`). The decider reads the `available balance` fold, runs the pack-rule checks and any accrual, and emits `HoldPlaced` / a refusal / `Movement` records; the folds label the resulting state.

### P4 — The posting/settlement feed is consumed, not owned

Already-cleared movements arrive as **`Observed` `Movement`s** ([ADR-PC-032 §A4](./ADR-PC-032-money-movement-primitive.md)) via the **settlement/posting-feed contract-shape ADR this epic owns** (bd `babelstone-30hf.5`). This family declares the `reason` strings its postings carry and folds them into the accounting balance and hold releases (D4); it **never** owns, restates, or hand-codes clearing/settlement/matching logic. A reversal or re-presentment arrives as an `Observed` reversing/late `Movement` on the same feed — recorded and reconciled (D4), not adjudicated. Gap-detection ownership on a feed the engine does not control is a slot of that sibling contract, not this ADR.

### P5 — Discovery is host assembly-scan; no per-family hand-edit

The family is discovered by the existing `FamilyModuleLoader` (folds) and `HostModuleLoader` (host wiring) assembly scans ([ADR-PC-021 §A13–§A14](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)). Wiring the family into the running host is its projects + the host `ProjectReference` (the scan anchor) + the authorize command surface and the account read/lifecycle endpoints + the pack pin, never a surgical edit to generic compose code. The Avro event schemas (`contracts/avro/accounts/current_account/`) and the CUE family schema (`contracts/cue/families/current-account.cue`, including the overdraft/limit constructs) land with the wiring; the concrete bus-promoted hold-event shapes are the **contract-reviewer**'s lane ([ADR-PC-033 slot 1](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md)).

### P6 — Legal holds & freezes are honoured through the generic fold, not re-decided

Where [ADR-PC-041](./ADR-PC-041-operation-constraining-legal-holds-and-freezes.md) legal holds / freezes apply, the generic available-balance fold already subtracts active legal holds; the authorize decider refuses a debit a freeze forbids **by reading the same fold**, without this family owning or amending the legal-hold contract. The distinction between authorization holds (`operations.Hold*`, capture-settled) and legal holds (`operations.FundsHeld`/`FundsReleased`, externally instructed) is [ADR-PC-041](./ADR-PC-041-operation-constraining-legal-holds-and-freezes.md)'s; this family instantiates the first, honours the second.

## Consequences

**What this choice makes easier:**

1. **The transactional-account abstraction is proven at n = 1 — the hub lands.** The demand account, the hub the other three families settle against, is delivered as the *first instance* of the fixed [ADR-PC-033](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) contract — the strongest evidence the abstraction is not secretly deposit-shaped, and the [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) v4 destination the account migrates onto.
2. **The card account (and later transactional families) reuse this, not co-invent it.** [ADR-PC-039](./ADR-PC-039-credit-card-family.md) already builds on this family's holds, available-balance fold, and authorize path (its `available credit = credit_limit − outstanding − Σ holds` is the same fold with the identity changed). The reconciliation policy and the overdraft pack constructs are reusable assets.
3. **Real-time authorization is native, not bolted on.** The authorize command is the [ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) surface under load ([ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)); idempotency, de-settlement, per-aggregate ordering, and the no-locking hold guarantee are reused as-is, and a decline is an auditable refusal fact.

**What this choice makes harder or impossible:**

1. **The account depends on the settlement/posting-feed contract being built (bd `babelstone-30hf.5`).** Until that `Observed`-`Movement` capture/posting feed lands, the account's everyday postings and hold captures have no substrate. The family declares its postings *as data* (`reason` + direction); it never hand-codes clearing. Accepted as the price of keeping the rails/scheme machinery outside the boundary.
2. **The engine is now a synchronous dependency on the payment hot path.** The authorize verdict's latency and availability are payment-path SLOs ([ADR-PC-034 §Consequences](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md)); [ADR-PC-001](./ADR-PC-001-event-store-technology.md) throughput and [ADR-PC-011](./ADR-PC-011-in-house-load-test-harness.md) load proof are load-bearing, not background. The async migration stays the reversible, load-driven scale-up path [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) reserved.
3. **The pack grammar widens** ([ADR-PC-006](./ADR-PC-006-cue-schema-language.md)/[ADR-PC-007](./ADR-PC-007-signed-yaml-oci-pack.md)) with the `arranged_overdraft_limit` and velocity/transaction constructs; the grammar must stay declarative (the same expansion [ADR-PC-030 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) and [ADR-PC-033 §Residual-risks](./ADR-PC-033-account-abstraction-and-hold-lifecycle.md) named).

## Residual risks

- **Hold reconciliation is correctness-sensitive.** Partial captures, late captures vs expiry (re-presentments), over-captures, and reversals arrive on a feed the engine does not control; the policy (D4) fixes each case and raises a reconciliation signal for the anomalous ones, but the *reconciliation signal handling* (who acts on it, how a mismatched account is resolved) is an operational surface adjacent to this family, not a fold guarantee. The fold stays pure; the reconciliation *workflow* is out of scope here.
- **The authorize-path availability targets are set when this family runs under load.** [ADR-PC-034](./ADR-PC-034-realtime-authorization-technique.md) fixed the *technique* but not the SLO numbers; the concrete latency/availability targets and DR posture under hot-path load ([ADR-PC-005](./ADR-PC-005-dr-rto-rpo.md)) are set when the family is built and [ADR-PC-011](./ADR-PC-011-in-house-load-test-harness.md) runs.
- **The overdraft accrual is a coarse-start.** v1 accrues overdraft interest/fees against a negative balance modelled like the loan's accrual; richer overdraft products (tiered rates, grace, fee-cap interplay) are additive fine-drift extensions, the same coarse-start discipline the loan schema took. The pack owns the values; a future pack field can carry the tier without reopening this Decision.
- **The dormant-account policy is deliberately simple.** `Dormant ⇄ Active` is a reversible non-terminal state; the *criteria* for dormancy (inactivity horizon) and the reactivation trigger are pack/product policy, not fixed in this ADR — additive within the same `LifecycleTransitions` table.
