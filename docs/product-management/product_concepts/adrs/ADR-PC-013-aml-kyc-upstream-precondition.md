## Context

[00 §4](../00-product-vision.md) is explicit on both halves of this boundary. On AML: "**Fraud and AML.** First-class systems in their own right, with their own vendors and their own regulators. The engine integrates with them; it does not absorb them." On KYC: "**KYC and onboarding.** A customer exists before they hold a product. KYC is upstream." The financial-crime systems are out of scope as products; the integration shape to them is in scope.

[Q-AU](../04-open-questions.md) frames the open work as a choice of contract shape, and names two:

- **(a) Async post-flag.** Constitution completes; an AML system reviews asynchronously; a **compensation flow** runs in the engine if AML later rejects.
- **(b) Sync pre-gate.** AML pre-gates constitution as a **synchronous check on the saga** ([integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md)) — the orchestrator dispatches a `ValidateClientEligibility` command to a Compliance adapter and waits for the verdict before proceeding.

This ADR introduces and adopts a **third shape (c): AML/KYC is an upstream precondition, and the engine is oblivious to the decision.** By the time a constitution request enters the engine, the financial-crime adjudication has already happened *elsewhere, before the request crossed the engine boundary*. The engine carries **no AML gate, no eligibility validation step, and no AML-reject compensation flow**, because the decision is not the engine's to make, gate on, or unwind.

### Why (c) is the consistent shape, not a novel one

The architecture already treats a peer regulatory precondition exactly this way. [integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md) handles the **PSD2 SCA precondition** at the edge: "The IAM must confirm that SCA has been completed for this session before the saga proceeds to any irreversible step. If SCA is absent or timed out, the edge returns `403` with reason `SCA_REQUIRED` — **the orchestrator never starts.**" The edge enforces the precondition from an upstream-issued signed assertion; the saga is oblivious to *how* SCA was satisfied and never re-adjudicates it. [integration_concepts §00](../../integration_concepts/00-introduction-and-decisions.md) reinforces the placement: edge pre-validations are "KYC OK?, sufficient balance?, product eligibility?" — named as **edge** concerns, not saga steps.

Shape (c) is "treat AML/KYC clearance the way the architecture already treats SCA." This makes it the *least* novel option, and it exposes the in-saga `ValidateClientEligibility` step in §05 as the actual inconsistency: a synchronous Compliance pre-check living inside the engine's write path is shape (b) embedded in the worked example, in tension with [00 §4](../00-product-vision.md)'s "the engine does not absorb AML/KYC" and with §05's own SCA treatment. **Adopting (c) therefore expunges that step** (see "What this expunges from the constitution saga" below).

### The three financial-crime touchpoints, separated

The decision is clean only once three distinct touchpoints are told apart — conflating them is what makes (a)/(b) look necessary:

1. **Onboarding KYC** — establishing the customer relationship. Upstream by definition ([00 §4](../00-product-vision.md): "a customer exists before they hold a product"). The engine never sees it.
2. **Per-constitution AML adjudication** — sanctions/PEP screening and source-of-funds checks evaluated *at the moment a specific product is constituted* (e.g. a large term deposit). This is the touchpoint (a)/(b) reach for. Under (c) it is enforced at the **edge as a precondition**, upstream of the engine's first irreversible step, exactly like SCA.
3. **Ongoing transaction monitoring** — behavioural/transaction AML over the life of the instance. This is genuinely downstream and is a **consumer of the engine's existing outbound event catalogue** ([02 §2.4](../02-v1-scope-term-deposits.md), governed by [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md)'s shape) — the same `DepositConstituted` fan-out that already lists a Compliance consumer in [integration_concepts §04](../../integration_concepts/04-plumbing-patterns.md). It is **post-flag by nature and never gates**, exactly as IFRS 9 is ([ADR-PC-015](./ADR-PC-015-ifrs9-signal-contract.md)).

Touchpoints 1 and 3 are uncontroversially out-of-engine. (c)'s contribution is to place touchpoint 2 with them — at the edge — rather than inside the saga.

## Decision

AML/KYC adjudication is an **upstream precondition** enforced **at the edge** ([ADR-IC-006](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-006-edge-api-gateway.md)), alongside the existing PSD2 SCA precondition. The engine's constitution saga carries **no eligibility/compliance validation step**, **no AML gate**, and **no AML-reject compensation flow**. The contract the engine commits to is therefore primarily a *negative* one (what the engine does **not** do) plus a thin inbound precondition; the six slots are filled in that light.

1. **Payload shape.** The contract is an **inbound precondition on the constitution request**, not a new event family. The `POST /v1/deposits` (constitution) request must arrive carrying an **upstream-issued clearance assertion** — a signed claim propagated the same way SCA completion is propagated ([integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md) step 0: "claims propagated as signed assertions"). The assertion reduces to an opaque envelope field:
   ```
   constitution request (edge)
     ...standard fields (client_id, amount, product_code, source_account, …)
     aml_clearance_ref       -- opaque token issued by the upstream AML/KYC authority
     aml_clearance_kind       -- closed code: ONBOARDING_KYC | TXN_SCREEN | COMBINED (engine never branches on it)
     aml_cleared_at           -- timestamp the upstream authority recorded clearance
   ```
   The engine records `aml_clearance_ref` (+ kind, + cleared-at) on the `ConstitutionRequested` event envelope **for audit lineage only**. **No AML/KYC event type is introduced**, and **no outbound AML signal** is defined beyond the existing [02 §2.4](../02-v1-scope-term-deposits.md) catalogue that ongoing monitoring already consumes. PII does not ride the assertion — `client_id` and `aml_clearance_ref` are references, not identity data ([ADR-PC-014](./ADR-PC-014-customer-notification-emit-contract.md) PII-by-reference, inherited unchanged).

2. **Semantics.** `aml_clearance_ref` asserts a *fact*: "an upstream authority cleared this client/relationship/transaction on financial-crime grounds before this request was issued." The engine treats it as **opaque provenance** — recorded, never parsed, never re-adjudicated. The *meaning* of clearance (what was screened, what counts as a hit, escalation, SAR/STR filing, the risk model) is **entirely upstream/counterparty-owned**. The engine holds **no screening list, no sanctions data, no PEP register, no risk score, no SAR workflow**. This is the same division [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md) and [ADR-PC-015](./ADR-PC-015-ifrs9-signal-contract.md) draw for outbound facts — the engine commits to recording the fact, the counterparty owns the verdict — applied here to an *inbound* fact.

3. **Ordering and delivery guarantees.** The precondition is **synchronous at the edge**: present-and-valid, or the edge returns `403` with reason `AML_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED` and **the orchestrator never starts** — byte-for-byte the SCA-precondition pattern ([integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md) step 0). Because the gate is an edge precondition rather than a saga participant, there is **no in-saga ordering or delivery concern for the gate** (there is no AML command, no AML reply, no AML compensation in the orchestration). For touchpoint 3 (ongoing monitoring), delivery is **at-least-once via the outbox/backbone, per-instance order preserved** — inherited verbatim from [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md), since monitoring is just another consumer of the business-event catalogue.

4. **Idempotency.** The precondition is enforced **once per constitution attempt** at the edge; `aml_clearance_ref` is recorded under the request's envelope `event_id`. There is **no separate dedupe surface** because the engine never calls AML — there is no AML effect to deduplicate. **Replay is trivially correct**: rebuilding from the event log re-presents the recorded `aml_clearance_ref` without any AML re-call, because none ever happened inside the engine. Ongoing-monitoring dedupe is consumer-side on `event_id`, inherited from [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md).

5. **Error model.** **The gate is upstream; the engine neither gates nor compensates.** This is the load-bearing slot and the reason both (a) and (b) are rejected:
   - **Absent/invalid clearance** is a `403 AML_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED` at the edge **before the saga starts**. The engine never constitutes an uncleared deposit, so it never has one to unwind.
   - **Shape (b) — sync in-saga gate — is rejected** because it couples constitution latency and availability to the AML system on the write path, and drags adjudication semantics (what is a hit, how to escalate, when to file a SAR) toward the engine — the out-of-scope absorption [00 §4](../00-product-vision.md) forbids. It is also redundant with an edge precondition that the architecture already runs for SCA.
   - **Shape (a) — post-flag with engine compensation — is rejected** because it requires the engine to **model and run an unwinding flow for a decision it has no authority over**, and it permits a window in which a not-yet-cleared deposit is live and settled to a current account. Unwinding a constituted, settled deposit is not a clean compensation; it is a reversal with real-world money movement and customer impact — precisely the irreversible-effect class the saga's reversibility ordering ([integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md), Primitive 6) is designed to keep *behind* a gate, not in front of one.
   - **Ongoing monitoring (touchpoint 3) is post-flag and never gates** — like IFRS 9 ([ADR-PC-015](./ADR-PC-015-ifrs9-signal-contract.md)). If monitoring flags an already-active deposit, any freeze/seizure is an **operations action expressed as ordinary engine commands** issued by an authorised actor (e.g. an account block, or an early-termination under legal order through the operations console, [integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md) "What This Concrete Saga Shows" §1) — **not** an engine-internal AML compensation. The engine executes a lawful command; it does not adjudicate the financial-crime decision behind it.

6. **Ownership and versioning.** The **edge/IAM + onboarding orchestration own** the clearance-assertion format and its issuance; the **AML/KYC systems own** the screening models, the sanctions/PEP data, the risk scoring, and the SAR/STR workflow. The **engine owns** only (i) the `aml_clearance_ref` / `aml_clearance_kind` / `aml_cleared_at` envelope fields it records for lineage, governed under [integration_concepts §08](../../integration_concepts/08-event-catalog-governance.md) / [§09](../../integration_concepts/09-long-term-schema-evolution.md), and (ii) the `403 AML_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED` edge contract ([ADR-IC-006](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-006-edge-api-gateway.md)). A breaking change to the clearance assertion is an edge/IAM concern, not an engine schema migration. A **consumer-driven contract test** (Pact, [ADR-IC-009](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-009-testing-infrastructure.md)) pins the claim the edge requires, so an upstream change that drops the clearance assertion breaks at CI, not at a production constitution.

### What this expunges from the constitution saga

Adopting (c) requires a corresponding change to the worked example in [integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md), so the narrative and this contract agree:

- The `PARALLEL_VALIDATION` fan-out drops from **three commands to two**: `ReserveAccountBalance` (Core ACL hold) and `ValidateProductLimits` (internal) remain; **`ValidateClientEligibility` (to the Compliance adapter) is removed**, along with its `EligibilityValidated` event, the `CMPL-HOLD` compliance hold, the `ConfirmRegistration` / `ComplianceRegistered` confirm step, and the `compliance_registration_id` that rode the public `DepositConstituted` event.
- The **reversibility-ordering pedagogy is preserved, not weakened**: the two remaining parallel steps are still reversible by construction (a Core hold and a pure computation), the irreversible Core debit still lands last, and the compensation scenarios are re-cast around the engine's *real* remaining failure modes — an early **balance-reservation / product-limit** rejection, and a late **post-debit activation** failure — instead of an eligibility rejection and a compliance-registration failure.
- **Product *limits* stay in the engine.** `ValidateProductLimits` ("does the client already hold N of this product; is the amount in range") is a product-engine rule bound to the pack, **not** an eligibility/financial-crime check. Only the AML/KYC/compliance adjudication moves upstream.
- The clearance gate is **relocated, not deleted**: it becomes an edge precondition next to the PSD2 SCA precondition (§05 step 0), enforced by [ADR-IC-006](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-006-edge-api-gateway.md).

### Legacy-seam / coexistence implications

[Q-AU](../04-open-questions.md) notes the shapes "have materially different cancellation semantics and different requirements on the legacy seam ([coexistence](../feature-design-strangler-fig-coexistence.md))." Shape (c) is the one with the cleanest seam:

- The clearance precondition sits at the **shared edge upstream of both cores**. During the strangler-fig coexistence period, a constitution is routed to the engine or to legacy by the [coexistence §3.1](../feature-design-strangler-fig-coexistence.md) date rule via the [ADR-PC-018](./ADR-PC-018-channel-routing-coexistence.md) gateway tier — and that **same edge** enforces clearance *before* routing. AML/KYC is therefore adjudicated **once, identically, regardless of which core the product lands on**.
- Contrast (b): an engine-resident AML gate would have to be **duplicated** on the legacy core, or legacy-routed products would **bypass** it — a split-brain compliance posture precisely in the period [coexistence §1](../feature-design-strangler-fig-coexistence.md) calls most fragile.
- Contrast (a): an engine-side AML-reject **compensation flow has no legacy analogue**, so the two cores would carry **asymmetric cancellation semantics** for the same financial-crime event. (c) makes cancellation semantics uniform: there is nothing for the engine to cancel, because clearance is a precondition above both cores.

## Consequences

**Easier.** The engine stays a product engine — no screening lists, no sanctions/PEP data, no risk model, no SAR workflow, no AML compensation flow — honouring [00 §4](../00-product-vision.md). The constitution saga is **simpler** (two-way validation fan-out, one fewer adapter, two fewer compensation cases). Cancellation semantics are **uniform across coexistence**. The contract reuses machinery that already exists: the SCA-style edge precondition for the gate, and the [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md) outbound catalogue for ongoing monitoring — **no new transport, no new AML event family, no new compensation path**.

**Harder / locked-in.** The gate **moves, it does not vanish**: the edge/onboarding orchestration must enforce clearance reliably before *every* constitution request, and the operating bank must ensure the upstream layer covers **per-constitution AML** (sanctions re-screen, source-of-funds for large deposits), not merely onboarding KYC. The engine commits the `aml_clearance_ref` envelope field as **audit lineage** under [§09](../../integration_concepts/09-long-term-schema-evolution.md) once it exists. The engine's correctness now **depends on an upstream contract it does not own** — mitigated by the CDC test on the edge precondition.

**Impossible by construction.** The engine cannot constitute an uncleared deposit (edge precondition fails closed). The engine cannot *be* the system that decides an AML outcome (it holds no model and records only an opaque token). An AML system's outage or rejection cannot **block or unwind** a committed engine deposit in a way that diverges from legacy (no in-engine gate, no compensation) — a monitoring-driven freeze is always a lawful operations *command*, never an engine financial-crime *judgment*.

## Residual risks

- **Per-constitution AML must actually be placed upstream — (c) assumes it, the bank must arrange it.** If the operating bank's onboarding clears only the *relationship* and expects *transaction-time* screening at product constitution, that screening must be positioned at the **edge precondition** (still upstream of the engine), not pushed back into the saga. This ADR fixes the *shape* (upstream precondition); *where exactly* in the upstream stack the transaction screen runs is an operating-bank deployment detail — a **production gate**, not a POC prerequisite, the same accept-now/gate-at-production posture as [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md) and [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md).
- **The clearance-assertion format and the edge `403` semantics are edge/IAM deliverables**, not engine deliverables ([ADR-IC-006](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-006-edge-api-gateway.md)). The engine fixes only the envelope field it records and the precondition it relies on.
- **Failure mode the contract permits.** A **misconfigured edge that fails to enforce the precondition** would let an uncleared request reach the engine, which would constitute it (it is oblivious by design). Mitigation: the precondition is a CDC-tested edge contract ([ADR-IC-009](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-009-testing-infrastructure.md)), and `aml_clearance_ref` is a **required envelope field** — a request lacking it fails the edge's light schema validation ([integration_concepts §05](../../integration_concepts/05-constitution-saga-walkthrough.md) step 0) and is rejected loud, not constituted silent.
- **The one scenario where (c) would yield to (b).** If a regulator or the operating bank mandates that the *product-constitution act itself* must synchronously consult AML **as part of the same transaction** in a way that cannot be lifted to an edge precondition, shape (b) returns for that case. The default is (c): every constitution-time screen the architecture has surfaced is expressible as an edge precondition, so the engine takes the upstream-precondition position and revisits only against a concrete mandate that proves the edge cannot host it. This is the mirror of [ADR-PC-015](./ADR-PC-015-ifrs9-signal-contract.md)'s posture (default to the clean shape; reconsider only if a named counterparty requirement forces it).
- **What this contract does not commit to.** The screening models, sanctions/PEP lists, risk scoring, the SAR/STR filing workflow, and the adjudication thresholds are **AML/KYC-team / operating-bank deliverables**. Reconciling a monitoring-driven freeze into engine state (a block or court-ordered early termination expressed as engine commands) is an **operations runbook**, not an engine compensation path — the engine supplies the lawful command surface; it does not supply the financial-crime decision behind the command.
