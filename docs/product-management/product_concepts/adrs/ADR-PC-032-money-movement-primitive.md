## Context

**In plain English.** babelstone moves money in a lot of places — a deposit takes principal *in*, a loan pays principal *out* at disbursement, a maturity pays out, an installment is collected. Today each of those is hand-written the same risky way: a family's decider calls a settlement port *synchronously*, and *only then* records the event. Because the money move and the record live in two different systems with no shared transaction, a crash in the gap leaves cash moved with **no record of it** — and because every family hand-codes this dance, each one re-decides "settle eagerly or route through a saga?" from scratch. This ADR introduces **one shared concept** — a `Movement`, a single recorded change of value against an account — so every family declares its money legs the same way, the record is always written *first*, and the cash movement becomes a safe, retryable consequence rather than an un-recoverable precondition.

**A note on the name.** The engine's atom is the noun **`Movement`**; *posting* is the **verb** — movements are *posted* to an account, and a balance is the fold over the movements posted to it. This keeps [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s posture ("a balance is a fold over postings", §95) literally true while reserving "posting" for the GL's act of composing balanced double-entry journal entries — which the engine delegates and never performs ([ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md)). A single-sided `Movement` carries no double-entry or chart-of-accounts baggage, and stays neutral about whether the cash has cleared — which is what lets one noun span both `origin`s below.

**The two problems, precisely.**

1. **An un-recoverable orphan window (bd `babelstone-5r9n.1`, HIGH).** A decider settles before it appends: `settlement.SettleAsync(Debit, principal)` then `AppendAsync(expectedVersion: -1)`. The append writes events + outbox rows in one transaction — but the settlement is a synchronous call to a *different* system (the legacy Core, across the ACL) and happens *before* that transaction. A failure between the two leaves money moved with no event. The orphan is in the **un-recoverable direction** ("cash gone, no record"). The eager `SettleAsync` also **bypasses** the ACL's double-debit guard ([ADR-IC-012 §P5](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-012-anti-corruption-layer-implementation.md)), so a retry can double-move.

2. **No shared money-leg concept (bd `babelstone-5r9n.2`).** Deposit *constitution* was deliberately de-settled onto a confirmation-gated saga leg (bd `babelstone-t7o3.4`, [ADR-PC-029 slot 2](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md)); the *remaining* deposit legs and *every* loan leg still settle eagerly. The asset-side disbursement reintroduced exactly the eager shape the deposit removed. Each family re-decides eager-vs-gated and re-hand-codes the leg — the per-operation fork bd `babelstone-t7o3.13` warns against ("deposit and loan settlement should share ONE parameterised confirmation-gated concept, not per-operation forks").

**The vocabulary already exists; the atom does not.** [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) fixed the posture: babelstone is "a core product & account ledger," and **"a balance is a fold over postings"** (§95) — i.e. over the movements posted to an account. But **no such type exists** — the atom a balance folds over was *named* and then *deferred* to the conta à ordem family (roadmap shape 4), and [ADR-PC-030 §P3/§176](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) deferred "the settlement/posting feed … a future **contract-shape ADR**." **This is that contract**, pulled forward two product shapes early because the settlement pain is already here. (ADR-PC-030 is Accepted as of 2026-06-23, alongside this ADR; this ADR discharges that deferred hook.)

**The boundary this crosses.** On the **Originated** side: the engine's append transaction on one side, the legacy Core's current-account journal on the other — bridged by the saga's gated settlement step ([ADR-PC-029](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) / [ADR-IC-003](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-003-saga-orchestrator.md)) delivering an [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) settlement command. On the **Observed** side: an upstream capture/settlement feed delivering already-cleared movements the engine records ([ADR-PC-030 §176](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)). The GL ([ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md)) is untouched — it still consumes raw business events and *posts* its *own* balanced journal entries; the engine never emits a GL posting.

**Scope — Path A only.** This ADR names **only the movement atom**: the single-sided `Movement`, the discipline that records it append-first, and the one gated leg that effects an Originated movement's cash. The full **`Account` abstraction** (every aggregate an account), the **accounting/available-balance split**, and the **hold lifecycle** (`HoldPlaced → HoldCaptured → HoldExpired`) remain [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s shape-3/shape-4 concern (`credit_card`, `conta à ordem`). The `Movement` defined here is the **forward-compatible atom those balances will fold over** — naming it now de-risks shape 4 rather than pulling its complexity forward.

## Decision

### Every money movement is a first-class `Movement` — a single-sided, append-first record of value moving against one engine-owned account. A decider produces movements as **data**; the spine records them in the *same transaction* as the event; an `Originated` movement's cash leg is effected by **one** generic confirmation-gated settlement leg; an `Observed` movement is recorded only. **No decider ever calls a settlement port synchronously before its append.**

The six contract slots:

1. **Payload shape.** The `Movement` value object is **single-sided** and **names no family**:
   - `account_ref` — the legacy-Core / engine-owned account identifier (an opaque reference, **never PII**, [ADR-PC-004](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)).
   - `direction` — `Debit | Credit`.
   - `amount` — `Money` (integer cents; crosses from `decimal` exactly once, [ADR-PC-010 §P2](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)).
   - `value_date` — the economic date (`valid_time`), never wall-clock.
   - `reason` — a closed, **family-declared** movement-code *string* (`disbursement`, `installment`, `maturity_payout`, …); an opaque token to the spine, **not** a family-typed shape.
   - `origin` — `Originated | Observed` (slot 2).
   - the append-idempotency `CommandId` (slot 4).

   A `Movement` is carried **as data inside the money-moving event's existing opaque self-describing payload** — **no new `events`-table column and no envelope change** ([ADR-PC-001 §P1](./ADR-PC-001-event-store-technology.md) / [ADR-PC-010 §P3](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md) column contract untouched). Because it rides the event, it is written **append-first inside the event's existing outbox transaction** ([ADR-IC-004](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-004-outbox-pattern-mechanism.md)). It is **not a balanced double-entry pair**: the engine records single-sided `Movement`s against its *own* account balances; **posting** them as a balanced journal entry against a chart of accounts is the GL's job ([ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md)), which the engine never does. The concrete Avro/CUE wire shape is the carrying event's own schema, governed like every emitted contract (EventCatalog + registry) and authored alongside the first implementing family — its wire detail is the **contract-reviewer**'s lane, deferred here, not redefined.

2. **Semantics.** A `Movement` asserts *"N cents moved `direction` against `account_ref` on `value_date`, because `reason`."* `origin` fixes who initiated it and what the spine does next:
   - **`Originated`** — the decider decided the movement (a loan disbursement, a deposit maturity payout). The `Movement` is recorded append-first; its cash leg is then **effected** on the legacy Core by the generic confirmation-gated settlement leg (slot 5) — the saga's gated `ReserveAccountBalance → ConfirmDebit` two-phase for a debit ([ADR-PC-029 slot 2](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md) / [ADR-IC-003](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-003-saga-orchestrator.md)), which delivers an [ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) `debitForConstitution`-class settlement command; a confirmation-gated credit for a payout ([ADR-PC-016 slot 5](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) — credits are always accepted but gated on confirmation). Settlement is a **consequence of a durable record, never a precondition of the append**.
   - **`Observed`** — an already-cleared movement (a card capture, a Core-side posting) arrives via the capture/settlement feed ([ADR-PC-030 §176](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)). The engine **records** it and folds it into the balance; there is **no cash leg to drive** (the money already moved upstream).

   The carrying lifecycle event still drives the aggregate's fold (a `LoanDisbursed` still moves the loan to `Active`); the `Movement` it carries is the **money-movement projection of that same fact** — the atom the (future) balance fold will sum. Available-balance and holds are **not** introduced here.

3. **Ordering and delivery.** **Per-account ordering**: movements against one `account_ref` are delivered in append order, inheriting the outbox's per-aggregate FIFO + advisory-lock relay ([ADR-IC-004](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-004-outbox-pattern-mechanism.md) — already guaranteed for events; the carried `Movement` rides it). The **Originated cash leg** is delivered **at-least-once** through the saga dispatcher ([ADR-IC-003](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-003-saga-orchestrator.md)), **effectively-once** at the Core via slot-4 idempotency. The **Observed feed** is at-least-once with consumer-side (engine) dedup. **Gap-detection** on the Observed feed is the feed owner's; on the Originated side it is the [ADR-PC-016 reconciliation flow 1](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) (every movement matched to a Core-journal counterpart).

4. **Idempotency — two distinct keys, not one.** The **engine append** is made idempotent by the **`CommandId`** the originating command already carries ([ADR-PC-029 slot 4](./ADR-PC-029-engine-command-ingress.md)): a replayed command id returns the original `commit_sequence` with no second append, so the `Movement` it records is never double-written. The **`Originated` cash leg** is made idempotent **by being a downstream saga step** rather than an eager in-engine call: it thereby inherits the ACL's **Core** idempotency key `(operation_type, saga_step_id, external_reference)` and the *refuse-to-send-unless-`RETRY_PERMITTED`* guard ([ADR-IC-012 §P4/§P5](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-012-anti-corruption-layer-implementation.md), which [ADR-PC-016 slot 4](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) inherits verbatim) — the **double-debit-prevented-by-construction** guarantee the eager `SettleAsync` *bypassed*. These are **two separate keys for two separate seams** (the engine append vs. the Core leg), owned by two parties (the engine receiver vs. the ACL); this ADR does **not** collapse or derive one from the other — the `Movement` carries the `CommandId` for the append + correlation, and the ACL owns the Core key. For an `Observed` movement the dedup key is the feed's movement id; the engine dedupes on it. **Uniqueness window**: the life of the instance (movements are immutable facts, retained like the events that carry them).

5. **Error model (the load-bearing slot).** Split by what is being recorded vs. effected:
   - The **`Movement` record is never gated** — it is part of the local append, committed atomically with the event. Recording the *fact* cannot fail independently of recording the event.
   - The **`Originated` cash leg is gated** ([ADR-PC-016 slot 5](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md)): the settlement leg blocks the **saga's** progress on Core confirmation — a refused debit (`InsufficientBalance`) compensates the leg; a non-confirmed debit enters the indeterminate-clearance protocol ([ADR-IC-012 §P5](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-012-anti-corruption-layer-implementation.md)); an unrecoverable failure parks in `HUMAN_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED` with **no compensation** ([ADR-IC-003 §P6](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-003-saga-orchestrator.md) — the money either moved or did not; the saga never invents an undo). Gating now blocks a **downstream saga step**, never the engine's **append** — so the bd `babelstone-5r9n.1` orphan window is **closed by construction**: the fact is always durable first, the cash leg always retryable.
   - The **GL signal stays post-flagged** ([ADR-PC-012 slot 5](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md), unchanged): a GL rejection never blocks or compensates the business flow.

6. **Ownership and versioning.** The **engine spine** owns the `Movement` type and the generic settlement-leg mechanism — **both name no family**: `Movement` carries an opaque `account_ref`, an opaque family-declared `reason` *string*, and generic `Money`/`direction`/`value_date`, exactly the shape the spine's existing `SettlementInstruction` / `ISettlementPort` already carry ([ADR-PC-021 §P1](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)); so `ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC` ([ADR-PC-021 §P2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)) holds and no family-typed shape lands in the engine set ([§A5](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)). Each **family** owns its `reason` vocabulary and which lifecycle events carry which movements (the family *content*, in `families/**`). The **legacy-Core / ACL team** owns the settlement wire contract ([ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md), inherited verbatim). The **capture/settlement-feed owner** owns the Observed feed schema. Breaking changes follow forward-only evolution ([ADR-IC-002](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-002-schema-format-and-registry.md) / [§09](../../integration_concepts/09-long-term-schema-evolution.md)) with a consumer-driven contract test (Pact, [ADR-IC-009](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-009-testing-infrastructure.md)) on each seam.

**Rejected: keep settling eagerly (status quo).** Leaves the orphan window open and the per-family fork unresolved — the two problems this ADR exists to close. **Rejected: a full `Account`/holds/available-balance model now.** Pulls [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) shape-3/4 complexity forward before any family needs it; this ADR names only the atom those balances will fold over (Path A). **Rejected: emit engine-side GL postings.** Would drag the chart of accounts and double-entry rules into the engine — exactly what [ADR-PC-012](./ADR-PC-012-gl-posting-signal-contract.md) rejected; the engine's `Movement` is single-sided and internal, the GL *posts* balanced journal entries, external.

## Consequences

**What this makes easier:**

1. **One settlement pattern, not per-family forks.** A family author declares a money leg *as data* and gets durability + idempotency + gating + `HUMAN_INTERVENTION_REQUIRED` parking for free — hand-coding none of it. This **is** bd `babelstone-t7o3.13`'s convergence, by construction.
2. **The disbursement-atomicity orphan is closed by construction** (bd `babelstone-5r9n.1`): the fact is always durable first; the cash leg is always a retryable downstream saga step inheriting the ACL's double-debit guard.
3. **The asset-side decision is recorded** (bd `babelstone-5r9n.2`): disbursement is one `Originated` `Movement` among many, not a bespoke eager fork.
4. **Forward-compatible with shape 4.** The `Movement` is the exact atom the `conta à ordem` / `credit_card` balances will fold over — naming it now de-risks [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s heaviest family.
5. **The single-sided direction ambiguity is resolved.** "A loan pays out, yet was coded `Debit`" stops being confusing once `account_ref` + `origin` make *whose* account and *which way* explicit.

**What this makes harder or impossible:**

1. **Every money-moving family pays a small uniform tax** — it must record a `Movement` on its carrying event. Accepted: it is the price of one pattern instead of N forks.
2. **The generic gated settlement leg must be built** in the orchestrator substrate as a **parameterised** module (direction + gating policy as data). Real platform work this ADR mandates: the saga substrate already provides the delivery/confirmation/parking machinery ([ADR-IC-003](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-003-saga-orchestrator.md)); what is new is parameterising the *leg semantics* so families stop hand-writing transition tables for it.
3. **Eager settlement becomes illegal**, even as a walking-skeleton shortcut. The append-first shape is the only legal one; the eager `SettleAsync`-before-append call sites (loan disburse/installment/early-repay + the deposit's remaining eager legs, bd `babelstone-t7o3.13`) are retired onto the leg.

## Residual risks (what this contract does **not** commit to)

- **The full `Account` abstraction, the accounting/available-balance split, and the hold lifecycle are not decided here** — they remain [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md) shape-3/4 (`credit_card`, `conta à ordem`). This ADR names only the movement atom.
- **The read side — a `Movement` projection / account-movement ledger — is named here but not decided.** This ADR fixes the *write-side* atom and its append-first discipline; *reading movements back* (an `account_ref`-keyed statement, the balance fold of [ADR-PC-030 §95](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)) is the read half of the deferred `Account`/available-balance concern (shapes 3–4). When it lands it should be **one spine-owned, `account_ref`-keyed projection — not a per-family copy** — because the `Movement` is family-agnostic, `Observed` movements have no owning family, and both reconciliation ([ADR-PC-016 flow 1](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md)) and the shape-4 balance fold are account-centric, not family-centric. Such a projection stays family-agnostic (it folds only the opaque `Movement`, never a family-typed shape), so [`ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`](./commitment-catalogue.md) holds. **Precondition (the real coupling):** a generic projector can fold movements off an arbitrary carrying event only if the `Movement` is **generically discoverable** — via a known payload slot or a family-declared seam (the `IErasable`-style pattern, [ADR-PC-021](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) / [ADR-PC-004](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)) — which slot 1's family-defined payload placement does **not** yet guarantee; defining that seam is the read model's precondition. Tracked as bd `babelstone-hkwp.1` (under the bounded shape-3-foundations epic `babelstone-hkwp` — the Account abstraction + Movement read/Observed completion PC-030/PC-032 defer), to be decided as a contract-shape ADR alongside the shape-3 `conta à ordem` family (v1 has no `Observed` producer, so no consumer needs it sooner).
- **The `Movement`'s concrete wire shape and the Observed feed schema are named, not authored** — they land with the first implementing family and are the **contract-reviewer**'s lane. v1 has **no** Observed producer, so the Observed path is **specified-but-dormant** until shape 3.
- **The "no shadow balance" rule is untouched** ([ADR-PC-016](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md) / [02 §3](../02-v1-scope-term-deposits.md)): through v1–v3 the legacy Core remains the authoritative owner of the *current-account* balance. The `Movement`s the engine records against an *engine-owned product* balance (deposit, loan) are its own, not shadows.
- **The real-time authorization technique** (sync vs async) stays [ADR-PC-030](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s deferred runtime ADR — nothing here commits it.
- **Each family's settlement-leg saga shape** (its states, its compensation, if any) is the family's to author within [ADR-IC-003](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-003-saga-orchestrator.md) discipline; this ADR fixes the *contract the legs share*, not each leg's lifecycle.

## Amendment — 2026-06-23: the generic-discoverability seam is a family-declared interface the spine calls, not a well-known payload slot

**In plain English.** This ADR named the `Movement` atom but deliberately left *where it lives on a carrying event* to each family's own payload (slot 1). That left a hole the read side cannot ignore: for a single, engine-owned projection to fold movements off events from *any* family, the spine must find the `Movement`(s) on an arbitrary event **without knowing that family's payload schema** — the precondition Residual-risk §2 flagged. Two shapes were on the table: a **well-known payload slot** every money-moving event must carry (a fixed key the spine reads out of the opaque JSON), or a **family-declared seam** the spine calls (the family says "here are my movements" through a small interface, exactly the way GDPR erasure already works through [`IErasable`](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)). This amendment decides the **family-declared seam**. It is additive — slot 1's "the `Movement` is carried as data inside the event's family-defined payload" is untouched; this only names *how the spine discovers it generically*, which slot 1 left open. (Decides bd `babelstone-hkwp.2`; resolves Residual-risk §2; the read-model precondition for bd `babelstone-hkwp.1`.)

### A1 · The seam: a family-declared `IMovementBearing` interface, folded by a spine-owned generic projector (refines §Decision slot 1 + §Residual-risks §2)

The spine discovers a carried `Movement` through a **family-declared seam**, not a reserved payload key. The shape mirrors the established cross-cutting fold seam ([ADR-PC-004 A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) `IErasable<TState>` / `CrossCuttingEventRegistrations.For<TState>()`): **the engine owns the generic mechanism; the family supplies only its own binding.** Concretely —

- A money-moving family event **declares** that it carries movements by implementing a spine-owned interface — `IMovementBearing` (a single member, `IReadOnlyList<Movement> Movements { get; }`, returning the movements the event already carries as slot-1 payload data). The interface lives in `Babelstone.Engine` beside `Movement`; it names no family, so [`ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`](./commitment-catalogue.md) ([ADR-PC-021 §P2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)) holds.
- A **spine-owned generic projector** folds the `account_ref`-keyed movement ledger by pattern-matching each replayed event on `IMovementBearing` and folding `evt.Movements` — it reads `Movement` (the family-agnostic atom) and the interface (a spine type), and **never** a family-typed event shape, so the projector stays family-agnostic exactly as [Residual-risk §2](#residual-risks-what-this-contract-does-not-commit-to) requires. (The projector itself is the read-model issue bd `babelstone-hkwp.1`; this amendment fixes only the **seam contract** the projector folds through, not the projection.)
- The **family** chooses which of its lifecycle events carry movements (the §Decision slot 6 ownership split is unchanged) and implements `Movements` on each — for a renewal, the same event returns both its rollover-debit and interest-credit `Movement`s, matching the `IReadOnlyList<Movement>` carrier the design companion (§2) already specifies. The engine knows "this event bears movements, fold them"; the family knows which facts move money.

### A2 · Why the seam, not a payload slot (the rejected alternative, recorded)

**Rejected: a well-known payload slot.** Reserving a fixed top-level key (e.g. `"movements"`) on every money-moving event's payload would force a **structural convention back into the opaque, self-describing payload** that [ADR-PC-028](./ADR-PC-028-event-store-payload-format.md) and [ADR-PC-001 §P1](./ADR-PC-001-event-store-technology.md) deliberately keep opaque to the spine (the spine keys events by the generic `family`/`event_type` columns and never parses inside the payload). It would also be **unenforceable family-agnostically**: nothing in the type system stops a family from omitting or misspelling the key, so the spine would silently fold zero movements off a non-conforming event — a correctness hole a `Planned` gate could not close without re-introducing payload-shape inspection into the spine. The interface is compiler-enforced at the family's binding site, matches the engine's one existing idiom for "engine owns the generic fold, family supplies its state/data" (`IErasable`), and keeps the payload opaque. The seam wins on every axis the §Decision already cares about.

### A3 · This amends slot 1; it does not supersede this ADR

§Decision (every money movement is a first-class `Movement`; append-first; the single Originated gated settlement leg; `Observed` recorded-only), slots 1–6, and the §Consequences all remain binding **as written**. Slot 1's "the `Movement` value object is single-sided, names no family, and is carried as data inside the money-moving event's existing opaque self-describing payload" is **untouched** — this amendment only names the **generic-discoverability seam** slot 1 left open (and Residual-risk §2 flagged), choosing the family-declared interface over the payload slot. The `Movement` carrier on a family event stays `IReadOnlyList<Movement>` (design companion §2); `IMovementBearing` is how the spine *reads* that carrier without naming the family. No commitment is reversed; the seam's correctness is exercised when the read-model projector (bd `babelstone-hkwp.1`) lands its gate.

## Amendment — 2026-06-23: the Observed capture/settlement feed contract — schema, `movement_id` dedup, gap-detection ownership

**In plain English.** Slot 2 names an `Observed` movement — one that already cleared upstream (a card capture, a Core-side posting) and arrives via a feed for the engine to *record only*, with no cash leg to drive. The original decision left that feed's wire shape, its dedup key, and who owns spotting dropped records to "the contract-reviewer's lane … when the first family migrates," and §Residual-risks called the whole `Observed` path **specified-but-dormant** (v1 has no `Observed` producer — a live one arrives with shape 3). This amendment fills the **contract shape** of that feed now — the dedup key, the recorded event, and gap-detection ownership — so the shape-3 `conta à ordem` family and its feed owner can build against one seam independently. It stays additive: it specifies the dormant `Observed` path the Decision already named; it changes no `Originated` behaviour and reverses no slot. (Decides bd `babelstone-hkwp.4`; fills slot 2/3 and [ADR-PC-030 §176](./ADR-PC-030-product-scope-and-boundary.md)'s deferred posting feed for the `Observed` direction. ### A4 · The recorded event: a cross-cutting `operations.MovementObserved`, family-agnostic, carrying one `Movement` with `origin = Observed` (fills slot 1 for the Observed direction)

An already-cleared movement is recorded as a **cross-cutting, engine-declared event** in the [`Babelstone.Engine.CrossCuttingEvents`](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) spine — provisionally `operations.MovementObserved` — the same family-agnostic posture as `PackVersionMigrated` / `PersonalDataErasureRequested` / the planned `LegacyInstanceObserved` ([ADR-PC-004 A4](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md) / [ADR-IC-017 §P4](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-017-integration-event-promotion-criterion.md)). It names no family ([`ENGINE_FAMILY_AGNOSTIC`](./commitment-catalogue.md) / [ADR-PC-021 §P2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md) holds), and it **implements `IMovementBearing` (the A1 seam)** — it carries the single already-cleared `Movement` (`origin = Observed`), so the **same** spine-owned movement projector (bd `babelstone-hkwp.1`) folds it into the `account_ref`-keyed ledger with **no special-casing** of the Observed source. The payload is structural only and **carries no PII** (`account_ref` is the opaque reference, never an IBAN; [ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)). Its concrete Avro shape is authored with the first live producer (shape 3) and is the **contract-reviewer**'s lane, exactly as §Residual-risks already deferred — what this amendment fixes is the *contract slots*, not the wire bytes.

### A5 · Dedup key, ordering, and gap-detection ownership (fills slots 3 + 4 for the Observed direction)

- **Idempotency / dedup key — the feed's `movement_id`.** This makes concrete what §Decision slot 4 already stated ("for an `Observed` movement the dedup key is the feed's movement id; the engine dedupes on it"). The feed assigns each cleared movement a stable `movement_id`; the engine dedupes **consumer-side**, in the ACL ingestion path, against its existing `inbound_event_dedup` store ([ADR-IC-012 §P1](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-012-anti-corruption-layer-implementation.md)) — the identical mechanism the [ADR-PC-017 batch ingest](./ADR-PC-017-legacy-batch-ingest-contract.md) uses for `LegacyInstanceObserved`, so a redelivered Observed movement produces **no** second `MovementObserved` event. Uniqueness window: the life of the instance (movements are immutable facts, retained like the events that carry them — §Decision slot 4, unchanged).
- **Ordering / delivery.** At-least-once with consumer-side dedup (§Decision slot 3, unchanged). Per-`account_ref` order is the engine's append order of the `MovementObserved` events it records; the feed makes no cross-account ordering promise. The movement ledger fold is order-insensitive for balance (a sum), so out-of-order arrival within an account self-heals on rebuild.
- **Gap-detection ownership — the feed owner's, reconciled against the ledger.** §Decision slot 3 already places Observed-feed gap-detection on the feed owner; this amendment names *how it is checked*: the engine's `account_ref`-keyed movement ledger (built from the cumulative `MovementObserved` stream) is reconciled against the feed owner's own record of cleared movements — the **same reconciliation-flow-2 invariant** ([ADR-PC-017](./ADR-PC-017-legacy-batch-ingest-contract.md) / [coexistence §7.3](../feature-design-strangler-fig-coexistence.md)) the batch feed already obeys: every movement the engine recorded must map to a cleared movement on the feed side, and vice versa. A divergence is the feed owner's to classify (dropped delivery vs an unrecorded upstream clearance); the engine surfaces the mismatch, it does not invent the missing fact. The `Originated` side keeps its own gap-detection — the [ADR-PC-016 reconciliation flow 1](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md), every movement matched to a Core-journal counterpart (§Decision slot 3, unchanged).

### A6 · Error model + ownership for the Observed direction; and this amends slots 1–4, it does not supersede this ADR

- **Error model (slot 5, Observed direction).** An `Observed` movement has **no cash leg to gate** (the money already moved upstream — §Decision slot 2). Recording it is part of a local append and **cannot be gated**; a malformed or non-conforming feed record is rejected fail-loud at the ACL ingestion boundary (it never half-records), the same posture as the [ADR-PC-017](./ADR-PC-017-legacy-batch-ingest-contract.md) file-level rejection. There is no compensation path because there is nothing to undo.
- **Ownership (slot 6).** The **capture/settlement-feed owner** owns the feed schema and gap-detection (§Decision slot 6, unchanged — restated concrete here); the **engine spine** owns the `operations.MovementObserved` record + its `IMovementBearing` fold; breaking changes follow forward-only evolution ([ADR-IC-002](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-002-schema-format-and-registry.md)) with a consumer-driven contract test ([ADR-IC-009](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-009-testing-infrastructure.md)) on the feed seam.
- **Additive.** §Decision slots 1–6 and §Consequences remain binding **as written**. Slot 2's "`Observed` … recorded only, no cash leg to drive" and slot 4's "the dedup key is the feed's movement id" are **untouched** — this amendment makes them buildable (names the recording event, the dedup mechanism, the gap-detection check, the error/ownership model) without reversing them. The `Originated` path is wholly unaffected. The feed remains **specified-but-dormant**: no v1 producer, no v1 consumer gate; the contract is here so the shape-3 producer and the engine build against one seam, and the `MovementObserved` Avro + its Pact land with that first producer.

## Amendment — 2026-06-24: the `Originated` cash leg carries the step-up-SCA freshness gate — the Movement-bearing event propagates the gateway-attested SCA claims to the settlement leg

**In plain English.** This ADR moved every irreversible cash leg off the engine onto an append-first settlement leg — but it said nothing about the **strong-customer-authentication (SCA) gate** an irreversible money-mover for an AI agent must pass. The engine's own step-up-SCA gate ([ADR-IC-010 §P8](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-010-mcp-server-runtime-and-sdk.md)) sat in front of the cash *because the engine moved the cash*; once this ADR makes the engine record append-first and effects the cash on a downstream `SettlementProcess` leg, that gate no longer sits in front of the money. The contradiction is concrete: [ADR-IC-010 §A10](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-010-mcp-server-runtime-and-sdk.md) (Accepted 2026-06-20) said a saga-routed money-mover threads the SCA proof "to the same engine gate," while this ADR (Accepted the next day) relocated the cash off the engine and was silent on SCA. This amendment fills that gap on the `Movement` side: the freshness gate rides the **`Originated` cash leg** (slot 5), and the gateway-attested SCA claims reach it on the Movement-bearing event's headers. It is **additive** — the append-first Decision, the single gated settlement leg, and the `Observed` recorded-only path are all unchanged; this adds a *gating input* to the leg slot 5 already gates on Core confirmation. (Co-amends [ADR-IC-010 §A11–§A12](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-010-mcp-server-runtime-and-sdk.md), which retires the "same engine gate" assumption; built by bd `babelstone-t7o3.15`, governed by bd `babelstone-t7o3.19`.)

### A7 · An `Originated` money-mover cash leg fail-closes on absent/stale step-up SCA, re-checked at the settlement-dispatch instant (refines §Decision slot 5)

For an `Originated` movement whose lifecycle event is an irreversible money-mover subject to step-up SCA ([ADR-IC-010 §P8](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-010-mcp-server-runtime-and-sdk.md) — a maturity payout, a coupon, an early-termination payout), the generic settlement leg (slot 5) carries a **freshness gate in addition to** its Core-confirmation gate:

- The gate is re-checked **at the settlement-dispatch instant**, against the same `SCA_MAX_AGE` window (300 s) the engine-direct gate and the Kong REST-route gate use ([ADR-IC-006 §P2](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-006-edge-api-gateway.md)). It is **not** inherited from saga entry: the saga duration ceiling (~1800 s, [ADR-IC-006 §P4](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-006-edge-api-gateway.md)) is 6× the window, so a proof fresh at entry can be stale at the cash instant. Absent or stale SCA refuses the leg fail-closed **before** `ConfirmDebit` / `ConfirmCredit` ([ADR-PC-016 slot 5](./ADR-PC-016-legacy-current-account-adapter.md)) — no cash moves; the leg parks for step-up / escalation rather than settling stale.
- **Attest, don't deny — at the substrate's seam too.** The orchestrator substrate **attests**: it re-emits the gateway-attested `X-SCA-Acr` / `X-SCA-Auth-Time` claims (carried on `saga_outbox` and re-emitted by the dispatcher, bd `babelstone-ls44`) onto the cash leg's delivery. The leg's **receiver** is the deny point. The substrate never denies on a domain/auth policy — it stays an extraction-ready, family-agnostic carrier ([ADR-PC-019 §P2](./ADR-PC-019-repository-strategy-monorepo.md) / [ADR-IC-018 §D2](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-018-family-owned-saga-modules.md)), exactly the attest-not-deny split [ADR-IC-006 §P2](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-006-edge-api-gateway.md) draws at the edge. The trust anchor stays the bank's AS-signed `acr` a courier (the agent) cannot forge ([ADR-IC-010 §A9](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-010-mcp-server-runtime-and-sdk.md)).

### A8 · The Movement-bearing event propagates the attested `acr` / `auth_time` to an event-auto-started leg; and this amends slots 1/5, it does not supersede this ADR

- **Propagation.** A standalone money-mover leg is **event-auto-started** (settlement design companion [§4.2](../feature-design-money-movement-settlement.md)) — there is no edge-originated saga command to carry the OIDC claims in (the seam bd `babelstone-ls44` fills for the *embedded* constitution path via the command sink). So for an auto-started leg the attested `acr` / `auth_time` propagate forward on the **Movement-bearing event's CloudEvents headers** — the operational-metadata channel, never the opaque payload and never PII ([ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md), the same posture `traceparent` and the `CommandId` take), captured from the original step-up at the engine boundary that appends the event. The `SettlementProcess` auto-started from that event reads the headers and re-checks freshness at dispatch (A7). The concrete header carriage lands with the settlement saga (bd `babelstone-t7o3.15`) and its gate (bd `babelstone-t7o3.19`); the `Observed` path carries no SCA (the money already moved upstream — slot 2).
- **Additive.** §Decision slots 1–6, §Consequences, and the A1–A6 amendments all remain binding **as written**. The append-first discipline (slot 5: "the `Movement` record is never gated … recording the *fact* cannot fail"), the single `Originated` gated cash leg, and "settlement is a consequence of a durable record, never a precondition of the append" are **unchanged** — this amendment adds a *freshness gating input* to the cash leg the slot already gates on Core confirmation; it does **not** gate the append. A new commitment (the settlement-leg analogue of [ADR-IC-010](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-010-mcp-server-runtime-and-sdk.md)'s `MCP_SCA_GATE_CANNOT_BYPASS`) is `Planned`, tracked by bd `babelstone-t7o3.19`, and registers in the [commitment catalogue](./commitment-catalogue.md) when that gate lands — the same `Planned`-until-the-gate-lands posture the §Verifiable-commitments rows already take.

## Amendment — 2026-06-26: a multi-direction Movement-bearing event fans out to one settlement instance per Movement (option b over one-event-per-Movement)

**In plain English.** A single event can record money moving two opposite ways at once — a deposit renewal rolls the principal over (a debit) **and** pays the interest (a credit) on the same append. A single settlement saga instance branches to ONE direction, so a two-direction event cannot be settled by one instance. This amendment fills that hole by deciding **how** a multi-direction event settles: the producer carries an **ordered list of every Originated direction** on the event, and the substrate fans that one event into **one settlement instance per Originated Movement**, each gated by its own direction. It is **additive** — slots 1–6, §Consequences, and amendments A1–A8 are all unchanged; this only resolves the multi-Movement *split* that slot 1 (the `Movement` carrier is `IReadOnlyList<Movement>`) and the §6/§95 ordering note ("a multi-`Movement` event effects its legs in declared order") already implied but left unbuilt. (Decides bd `babelstone-t7o3.21`; surfaced completing bd `babelstone-t7o3.20`.)

### A9 · The chosen model is per-Movement header carriage with one `SettlementProcess` instance per Originated Movement (option b), NOT one-event-per-Movement (option a)

Two models were on the table for a genuinely multi-direction event:

- **(a) one event per Movement** — the family splits the renewal's two legs across two separate appends/events, each single-direction. **Rejected:** it breaks the append-first **atomicity** the Decision rests on (slot 5: "recording the `Movement` is part of the local append") — both Movements are recorded in ONE transaction, so splitting them across two appends reopens exactly the orphan-window class this ADR closes, and it pushes family-specific event-splitting logic into every producer (against the family-agnostic spine, [ADR-PC-021 §P2](./ADR-PC-021-application-layer-family-owned-deciders.md)).
- **(b) per-Movement header carriage + one settlement instance per Movement** — the family keeps emitting **one** event carrying its `IReadOnlyList<Movement>` (slot 1, unchanged); the substrate fans that one event into N settlement instances, each gated by its own direction. **Chosen.**

Concretely, the engine-spine producer ([`Babelstone.Engine.MovementHeaders`](../../../../engine/src/Babelstone.Engine/MovementHeaders.cs)) emits `ce_movementorigin = Originated` and a **single ordered `ce_movementdirections` list** — the comma-separated closed-enum names of every Originated direction in **carrier order**, ONE entry per Movement: a one-entry list for a standalone leg (disbursement, maturity, coupon, early-termination — e.g. `Credit`), an N-entry list for a multi-direction event (e.g. `Debit,Credit`). There is no separate single-direction header: the substrate's fan-out is the uniform path, taking a one-entry list to exactly one instance and an N-entry list to N (the fail-loud guard the multi-direction split previously raised is replaced by this list). Because the list carries **one entry per Movement** — not per *distinct* direction — two same-direction Movements (two debits) settle as **two** instances, the per-Movement semantics this amendment names, not the one the earlier first-direction-only sketch would have silently collapsed to one. The values stay closed-enum member NAMES only — no amount, no `account_ref`, no PII ([ADR-PC-004 §P2](./ADR-PC-004-pii-crypto-shredding.md)).

### A10 · The substrate fans the list into deterministic per-Movement subjects; per-account FIFO and effectively-once hold; and this amends slots 1/2/5, it does not supersede this ADR

- **Fan-out.** The substrate-owned settlement module reads `ce_movementdirections` and starts **one `SettlementProcess` instance per entry**, in carrier order, each driven by its own single-entry `ce_movementdirections` list — the fan-out reduces each leg's list to its one direction, which the established `IEventSubstitutor` resolves to that leg's debit/credit branch ([ADR-IC-018 §D5](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-018-family-owned-saga-modules.md), the substrate reads headers, never the payload). The **primary** instance (a one-entry list, or the first entry of a multi) keeps the event's own `ce_subject` as its `process_id` (the standalone single-direction path is unchanged); each **secondary** instance's `process_id` is a **deterministic** v5-style derivation of `(ce_subject, index)`, and its dedup message id a v5-style derivation of `(message_id, index)` — never `Guid.NewGuid()` ([ADR-PC-010 §P5](./ADR-PC-010-dotnet-hand-rolled-engine.md)). So a redelivery re-derives the SAME subjects and dedup ids: the auto-start `process_id` PK and the inbox dedup row each collide, and every leg starts and advances **exactly once** (effectively-once per leg). All legs are born **atomically** in the consume loop's one transaction with the offset commit — all-or-nothing.
- **Per-account FIFO.** Each instance gets its own `process_id`, hence its own dispatcher per-`process_id` FIFO lane ([ADR-IC-004](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-004-outbox-pattern-mechanism.md) / bd `babelstone-t7o3.7`), and the legs are emitted in carrier order, so a renewal's debit and credit dispatch in declared order (§6/§95).
- **Additive.** §Decision slots 1–6, §Consequences, and amendments A1–A8 all remain binding **as written**. Slot 1's `Movement` carrier (`IReadOnlyList<Movement>`, single-sided, family-named-nothing) is **untouched** — this only names how the substrate *settles* a carrier with movements in more than one direction; slot 2's Originated-has-a-cash-leg and slot 5's single gated leg per Movement are **unchanged** (each fanned-out instance IS that single gated leg). No commitment is reversed. The `MOVEMENT_CASH_LEG_IDEMPOTENT` gate (catalogue row MOVEMENT-2) stays `Live` and now also covers the multi-direction fan-out; no new commitment is introduced by this amendment.

*Revised 2026-07-04 (bd `babelstone-3o6m`; resolves open question Q-BH): the §A9/§A10 derived-subject scheme now covers **per-occurrence settlement identity**, not just redelivery and multi-direction fan-out. In plain English: one saga per account was one saga per LIFETIME — once a loan's first cash leg completed its terminal saga, installment 2's leg could neither start (the `ON CONFLICT (process_id)` start collided with occurrence 1's row) nor park (the terminal saga no-ops every later event), so a recurring product's later movements were settled nowhere. Now **every** `SettlementProcess` instance's `process_id` — the primary included — is a deterministic v5-style derivation of `(ce_subject, event id, movement index)`; the "primary keeps the event's own `ce_subject`" clause above is retired (the primary still keeps the event's own dedup message id). Same inputs → same ids, so a redelivery still collides on the `saga_state` PK and the inbox dedup (effectively-once per leg, unchanged); a LATER event on the same subject (its own `ce_id`) derives a fresh instance, so `SETTLEMENT_COMPLETED` stays terminal **per occurrence** and occurrence N ≥ 2 has its own saga to drive or park — exactly the recurring surface [ADR-PC-036 §Decision 4](./ADR-PC-036-lifecycle-command-driver.md)'s LCD-2 gate reads. The account/instrument linkage moves to a new, indexed, NOT NULL `saga_state.subject_id` column (orchestrator migration `0009`, forward-only per [ADR-PC-001 §P5](./ADR-PC-001-event-store-technology.md); backfilled `subject_id := process_id`, which is exactly the pre-revision identity), carried on the fan-out projection and persisted by both `SagaStateStore` start paths (an edge-started saga's `subject_id` = its `process_id`). Because the ACL idempotency references derive from the process id ([ADR-IC-012 §P4](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-012-anti-corruption-layer-implementation.md); `SettlementReferences`), per-occurrence process ids yield per-occurrence `external_reference` tokens — installment 2's debit can never dedup against installment 1's — while re-emission for the SAME occurrence stays byte-stable. Still additive: slots 1–6, §Consequences, and A1–A8 remain binding as written; the fan-out stays header-only and family-agnostic ([ADR-IC-018 §P5](../../integration_concepts/adrs/ADR-IC-018-family-owned-saga-modules.md)); MOVEMENT-2 stays `Live` and now also covers per-occurrence identity (pinned by the fan-out unit tests and the auto-start integration test's completed-then-next-occurrence walk).*
