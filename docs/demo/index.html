The problem

Core banking is a
haunted house.

Legacy cores are opaque and batch-driven — and terrifying to change.

A rounding error in interest or tax isn't a bug — it's a regulatory event and real money lost.

So nobody fully trusts the numbers — and nobody dares touch the system.

The insight

Immutable ledger.
Pure math.

Every change is an immutable, replayable fact. The financial kernel is pure — no clock, no I/O, no randomness.

Replay the whole bank → byte-identical state. Trust comes from determinism, not faith.
